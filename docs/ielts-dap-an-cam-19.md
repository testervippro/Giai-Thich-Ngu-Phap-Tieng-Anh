### 📖 Passage 1: How tennis rackets have changed

#### Câu 1–7 · TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN

> 💡 **Cách làm:** Tìm đúng câu chứa thông tin (từ khoá ít đổi: tên riêng, số, năm), rồi so **ý**: cùng ý → TRUE; ngược ý (phủ định, đổi số/so sánh, all↔some) → FALSE; bài không nói tới điểm mấu chốt → NOT GIVEN. Thứ tự câu hỏi đi theo thứ tự bài. · [xem mẹo dạng này](ielts-dap-an-tong-quan.md?id=tfng)

- **1.** People had expected Andy Murray to become the world’s top tennis player for at least five years before 2016.
  → ✅ **FALSE**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 1 của bài):* In **2016**, the British professional **tennis** **player** **Andy** **Murray** was ranked as the **world**’s number one.
- **2.** The change that Andy Murray made to his rackets attracted a lot of attention.
  → ✅ **FALSE**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 1 của bài):* In 2016, the British professional tennis player **Andy** **Murray** was ranked as the world’s number one.
- **3.** Most of the world’s top players take a professional racket stringer on tour with them.
  → ✅ **NOT GIVEN**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 10 của bài):* ‘**Touring** **professionals** have their **rackets** customised to their specific needs,’ says Colin Triplow, a UK-based **professional** **racket** **stringer**.
- **4.** Mike and Bob Bryan use rackets that are light in comparison to the majority of rackets.
  → ✅ **FALSE**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 13 của bài):* Take the US professional tennis players **Mike** and **Bob** **Bryan**, for example: ‘We’re very particular with our **racket** specifications,’ they say.
- **5.** Werner Fischer played with a spaghetti-strung racket that he designed himself.
  → ✅ **NOT GIVEN**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 20 của bài):* **Racket** modifications mainly date back to the 1970s, when the amateur German tennis **player** **Werner** **Fischer** started **playing** with the so-called **spaghetti**-strung **racket**.
- **6.** The weather can affect how professional players adjust the strings on their rackets.
  → ✅ **TRUE**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 37 của bài):* For example, much of the serving power of US **professional** **player** Pete Sampras was attributed to the addition of four to five lead weights onto his **rackets**, and today many **professionals** have the weight **adjusted** during the manufacturing process.
- **7.** It was believed that the change Pete Sampras made to his rackets contributed to his strong serve.
  → ✅ **TRUE**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 37 của bài):* For example, much of the **serving** power of US professional player **Pete** **Sampras** was attributed to the addition of four to five lead weights onto his **rackets**, and today many professionals have the weight adjusted during the manufacturing process.
