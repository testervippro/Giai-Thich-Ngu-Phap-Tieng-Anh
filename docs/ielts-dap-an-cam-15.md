### 📖 Passage 1: Nutmeg – a valuable spice

#### Câu 1–4 · Điền ghi chú (Note completion)

> 💡 **Cách làm:** Ghi chú đi theo thứ tự bài; đoán từ loại trước, chép **nguyên văn** và đúng giới hạn số từ. · [xem mẹo dạng này](ielts-dap-an-tong-quan.md?id=notes)

- **1.** ● the leaves of the tree are 1 ……………………. in shape
  → ✅ **oval**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 3 của bài):* The **tree** is thickly branched with dense foliage of tough, dark green **oval** leaves, and produces small, yellow, bell-**shaped** flowers and pale yellow pear-**shaped** fruits.

  → ✅ **husk**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 5 của bài):* When the **fruit** is **ripe**, this **husk** splits into two halves along a ridge running the length of the **fruit**.
- **3.** ● the 3 ……………………. is used to produce the spice nutmeg
  → ✅ **seed**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 7 của bài):* These are the sources of the two **spices** **nutmeg** and mace, the former being **produced** from the dried **seed** and the latter from the aril.
- **4.** ● the covering known as the aril is used to produce 4 ……………………..
  → ✅ **mace**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 7 của bài):* These are the sources of the two spices nutmeg and **mace**, the former being **produced** from the dried seed and the latter from the **aril**.

#### Câu 5–7 · TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN

> 💡 **Cách làm:** Tìm đúng câu chứa thông tin (từ khoá ít đổi: tên riêng, số, năm), rồi so **ý**: cùng ý → TRUE; ngược ý (phủ định, đổi số/so sánh, all↔some) → FALSE; bài không nói tới điểm mấu chốt → NOT GIVEN. Thứ tự câu hỏi đi theo thứ tự bài. · [xem mẹo dạng này](ielts-dap-an-tong-quan.md?id=tfng)

- **5.** In the Middle Ages, most Europeans knew where nutmeg was grown.
  → ✅ **FALSE**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 8 của bài):* **Nutmeg** was a highly prized and costly ingredient in European cuisine in the **Middle** **Ages**, and was used as a flavouring, medicinal, and preservative agent.
- **6.** The VOC was the world’s first major trading company.
  → ✅ **NOT GIVEN**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 17 của bài):* In 1602, Dutch merchants founded the **VOC**, a **trading** corporation better known as the Dutch East India **Company**.
- **7.** Following the Treaty of Breda, the Dutch had control of all the islands where nutmeg grew.
  → ✅ **TRUE**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 35 của bài):* After decades of fighting for **control** of this tiny **island**, the **Dutch** and British arrived at a compromise settlement, the **Treaty** of **Breda**, in 1667.

#### Câu 8–13 · Điền bảng (Table completion)

> 💡 **Cách làm:** Xác định cột/hàng → biết cần loại thông tin gì (năm, tên, số…); chép **nguyên văn**, đúng giới hạn số từ. · [xem mẹo dạng này](ielts-dap-an-tong-quan.md?id=table)

- **8.** Nutmeg was brought to Europe by the 8 ……………
  → ✅ **Arabs**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 9 của bài):* Throughout this period, the **Arabs** were the exclusive importers of the spice to **Europe**.
- **9.** Demand for nutmeg grew, as it was believed to be effective against the disease known as the 9 ……………
  → ✅ **plague**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 20 của bài):* At the same time, thousands of people across Europe were dying of the **plague**, a highly contagious and deadly **disease**.

  → ✅ **lime**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 32 của bài):* In addition, all exported **nutmeg** was covered with **lime** to make sure there was no chance a fertile seed which could be grown elsewhere would leave the **islands**.
- **11.** – finally obtained the island of 11 …………… from the British
  → ✅ **Run**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 36 của bài):* Intent on securing their hold over every nutmeg-producing **island**, the Dutch offered a trade: if the **British** would give them the **island** of **Run**, they would in turn give Britain a distant and much less valuable island in North America.
- **12.** 1770 – nutmeg plants were secretly taken to 12 ……………
  → ✅ **Mauritius**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 40 của bài):* Then, in **1770**, a Frenchman named Pierre Poivre successfully smuggled **nutmeg** **plants** to safety in **Mauritius**, an island off the coast of Africa.
- **13.** 1778 – half the Banda Islands’ nutmeg plantations were destroyed by a 13 ……………
  → ✅ **tsunami**
  > 📍 *Dẫn chứng (câu 42 của bài):* Next, in **1778**, a volcanic eruption in the **Banda** region caused a **tsunami** that wiped out **half** the **nutmeg** groves.
