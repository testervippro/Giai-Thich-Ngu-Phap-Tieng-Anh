## 🧭 Cách làm từng dạng (kèm ví dụ thật từ đề)

<a id="tfng"></a>

### TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN <small>(336 câu)</small>

💡 Tìm đúng câu chứa thông tin (từ khoá ít đổi: tên riêng, số, năm), rồi so **ý**: cùng ý → TRUE; ngược ý (phủ định, đổi số/so sánh, all↔some) → FALSE; bài không nói tới điểm mấu chốt → NOT GIVEN. Thứ tự câu hỏi đi theo thứ tự bài.

- *(Cam 10 · Test 1 · câu 3)* The few existing stepwells in Delhi are more attractive than those found elsewhere. → ✅ **NOT GIVEN**
  > 📍 Most **stepwells** are found dotted around the desert areas of Gujarat (where they are called vav ) and Rajasthan (where they are known as baori ), while a few also survive in **Delhi**.
- *(Cam 10 · Test 1 · câu 5)* The number of steps above the water level in a stepwell altered during the course of a year. → ✅ **TRUE**
  > 📍 As their name suggests, **stepwells** comprise a series of stone **steps** descending from ground **level** to the **water** source (normally an underground aquifer) as it recedes following the rains.
- *(Cam 10 · Test 1 · câu 23)* To reduce production costs, some industries have been moved closer to their relevant consumers. → ✅ **FALSE**
  > 📍 This phenomenon has been emphasised by the relocation of some industries, particularly those which are labour intensive, to **reduce** **production** **costs**, even though the **production** site is hundreds or even thousands of kilometres away from the final assembly plant or away from users.

### YES / NO / NOT GIVEN <small>(157 câu)</small>

💡 Như TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN nhưng so với **quan điểm / nhận định của tác giả** (chú ý từ thể hiện thái độ: surprisingly, unfortunately, it is clear that…). Thứ tự câu hỏi đi theo thứ tự bài.

- *(Cam 10 · Test 2 · câu 36)* Art history should focus on discovering the meaning of art using a range of media. → ✅ **NOT GIVEN**
  > 📍 Consequently, the dominant critical approach becomes that of the **art** historian, a specialised academic approach devoted to ‘**discovering** the **meaning**’ of **art** within the cultural context of its time.
- *(Cam 10 · Test 2 · câu 37)* The approach of art historians conflicts with that of art museums. → ✅ **NO**
  > 📍 Consequently, the dominant critical **approach** becomes that of the **art** **historian**, a specialised academic **approach** devoted to ‘discovering the meaning’ of **art** within the cultural context of its time.
- *(Cam 10 · Test 2 · câu 39)* Reproductions of fine art should only be sold to the public if they are of high quality. → ✅ **NOT GIVEN**
  > 📍 If appropriate works of **fine** **art** could be rendered permanently accessible to the **public** by means of **high**-fidelity **reproductions**, as literature and music already are, the **public** may feel somewhat less in awe of them.

### Chọn tiêu đề cho đoạn (Matching headings) <small>(106 câu)</small>

💡 Đọc câu chủ đề (thường câu 1–2) và câu kết của đoạn, tìm **ý chính cả đoạn**, không chọn heading chỉ khớp một chi tiết. Làm đoạn dễ trước, loại dần heading đã dùng.

### Nối thông tin với đoạn (Matching information) <small>(189 câu)</small>

💡 Thông tin cần tìm thường là **chi tiết** (một ví dụ, một lý do, một con số…) nằm ở bất kỳ đoạn nào, không theo thứ tự. Gạch từ khoá dạng danh từ của câu hỏi rồi quét từng đoạn; một đoạn có thể dùng nhiều lần.

- *(Cam 14 · Test 2 · câu 14)* why some people avoided hospitals in the 19th century → ✅ **F**
  > 📍 Much of the ingenuity present in **19th**-**century** **hospital** and building design was driven by a panicked public clamouring for buildings that could protect against what was thought to be the lethal threat of miasmas – toxic air that spread disease.
- *(Cam 14 · Test 2 · câu 16)* a comparison between the circulation of air in a 19th-century building and modern standards → ✅ **E**
  > 📍 ‘We discovered that **19th**-**century** hospital wards could generate up to 24 **air** changes an hour – that’s similar to the performance of a **modern**-day, computer-controlled operating theatre.
- *(Cam 14 · Test 2 · câu 17)* how Short tested the circulation of air in a 19th-century building → ✅ **D**
  > 📍 **Short**’s book highlights a developing and sophisticated art and science of ventilating **buildings** through the **19th** and earlier-20th centuries, including the design of ingeniously ventilated hospitals.

### Nối nửa câu (Sentence endings) <small>(52 câu)</small>

💡 Đọc nửa đầu, tìm vị trí trong bài (theo thứ tự), rồi chọn kết thúc vừa **đúng ý** vừa **đúng ngữ pháp**.

  > 📍 One possibility is that these traits are lost and then simply reappear, in much the same way that similar structures can independently arise in unrelated **species**, such as the dorsal fins of **sharks** and **killer** **whales**.
- *(Cam 11 · Test 3 · câu 22)* Arctic terns illustrate migrating animals’ ability to → ✅ **E** — ignore distractions.
  > 📍 The **arctic** **tern** resists **distraction** because it is driven at that moment by an instinctive sense of something we humans find admirable: larger purpose.

### Nối đặc điểm / người (Matching features) <small>(144 câu)</small>

💡 Tìm **tên người / mốc thời gian** trong bài trước (dễ quét), đọc quanh tên đó rồi so với các nhận định. Không theo thứ tự; một lựa chọn có thể dùng nhiều lần.

  > 📍 For example, a very **close** positive relationship was found when **children**’s IQ scores were compared with their home educational provision (**Freeman**, 2010).

  > 📍 Psychologist **Todd** **Kashdan** has this advice for those **people** taking up a **new** passion: ‘As a **newcomer**, you also have to tolerate and laugh at your own ignorance.
