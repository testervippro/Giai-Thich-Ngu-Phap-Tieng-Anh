# Cấu trúc ngữ pháp hay gặp trong TOEIC Reading (quét từ đề thật)

> Quét toàn bộ [toeic-ets-2026-reading.md](toeic-ets-2026-reading.md) (10 đề Part 5-7) để tìm các **cấu trúc/collocation ngữ pháp lặp lại** — không chỉ từ vựng đơn lẻ mà cả khung câu (verb/adj + giới từ, verb + to V/V-ing, liên từ cặp...). Mỗi cấu trúc có **ví dụ thật trích từ đề** để thấy cách dùng trong ngữ cảnh thực tế, không phải câu tự đặt.

> Đây là các cấu trúc **xuất hiện ít nhất 1 lần thật trong 10 đề** — ưu tiên học vì khả năng gặp lại trong đề thi thật cao hơn học ngữ pháp dàn trải.

---

## Động từ + giới từ

| Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ trong đề |
|---|---|---|
| **comply with** | tuân thủ | The job involves editing engineering reports to ensure they meet quality specifications and comply with industry standards. |
| **consist of** | bao gồm, gồm có | The facility consists of four rooms, of which is designed to represent a different geographic area that is part of Crystal's global operations. |
| **apply for** | nộp đơn xin (việc/học bổng...) | Do not forget to mention the specific job you are applying for and explain why you would be a good fit. |
| **focus on** | tập trung vào | It focuses on protecting the environment. |
| **rely on** | dựa vào, tin cậy vào | When crafting a personal statement, provide concrete examples of times when you had to rely on your unique qualities. |
| **participate in** | tham gia vào | Gardens selected to participate in the tour must be within Presidio County and include only plants native to the region. |
| **invest in** | đầu tư vào | Chilean Smelting, Inc., a major producer, has invested in improved equipment and aims to increase production. |
| **specialize in** | chuyên về | I specialize in floral design. |
| **enroll in** | ghi danh, đăng ký học | Enroll in our double-rewards program on Avanti's mobile or desktop app. |

## Tính từ + giới từ

| Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ trong đề |
|---|---|---|
| **responsible for** | chịu trách nhiệm về | Departments will be responsible for picking up and distributing their own mail. |
| **aware of** | nhận thức được, biết về | Because we take safety seriously, we urge any employee who becomes aware of a breach of these protocols to contact a supervisor or the safety coordinator. |
| **familiar with** | quen thuộc với | However, customers may not be familiar with our intensive special cleaning services. |
| **pleased with** | hài lòng, vui vì | She is pleased with the device she uses. |
| **interested in** | quan tâm đến, hứng thú với | If you are interested in participating, please call me at 020 7946 0532. |
| **committed to** | cam kết với/dốc sức cho | We are committed to baking Muffin Lady Muffins authentically with only the finest ingredients. |

## Cụm giới từ cố định

| Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ trong đề |
|---|---|---|
| **in addition to** | ngoài ra, thêm vào đó | Do you think Ms. Mayer would want to see a cover letter in addition to my résumé? |
| **according to** | theo (nguồn tin/ai đó) | According to Ms. Grewal, the airline has scheduled its inaugural flight from Arlford Regional Airport for 1 August. |
| **with regard to / regarding** | liên quan đến, về việc | A decision regarding added routes is expected by 31 December. |
| **in accordance with** | phù hợp với, theo đúng | All personally identifiable information will be treated confidentially in accordance with the Lorne privacy statement, which can be found at lornettransportation.com.au. |
| **on behalf of** | thay mặt cho | Dear Ms. Inoue, I am writing on behalf of Impressionise. |

## Động từ + to V

| Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ trong đề |
|---|---|---|
| **decide to** | quyết định làm gì | So, in November, we decided to try a PUW machine. |
| **plan to** | dự định, lên kế hoạch làm gì | It plans to open physical stores. |
| **intend to** | có ý định làm gì | I'm finally ready to slow down." Ellison intends to spend more time in her garden. |
| **hope to** | hy vọng làm gì | We hope to see you there! |
| **expect to** | mong đợi/dự kiến làm gì | The new phones are expected to be on store shelves in November. |
| **offer to** | đề nghị làm gì | Finally, on Saturday, 500 tree saplings will be offered to residents free of charge. |
| **fail to** | không làm được, thất bại trong việc | However, this method was ineffective because the bags were rather flimsy, thus failing to prevent water leakage. |
| **manage to** | xoay xở/cố gắng làm được | As it turned out, I managed to contact them at just the right time. |
| **aim to** | nhắm tới việc làm gì | Chilean Smelting, Inc., a major producer, has invested in improved equipment and aims to increase production. |

## Tính từ + to V

| Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ trong đề |
|---|---|---|
| **be able to** | có thể làm gì | Let me know by 12 October whether you will be able to attend. |
| **be likely to** | có khả năng sẽ làm gì | We will instead be using Curtain Call, which we trust will prove to be a more reliable program that is less likely to crash. |
| **be eager to** | háo hức làm gì | I know you must be eager to rehearse in the newly refurbished building next week. |
| **be willing to** | sẵn lòng làm gì | Are you willing to put your education, your experience, and your imagination to great use? |
| **be entitled to** | được quyền (hưởng) gì | You are even entitled to a free scoop at any Brice's Ice Creamery on your birthday. |
| **be required to** | được yêu cầu/bắt buộc làm gì | A minimum deposit of FRW 100,000 is required to open a BASA, while a monthly administration fee of FRW 1,500 is charged. |
| **be permitted to** | được phép làm gì | Its staff are permitted to work from home. |
| **be encouraged to** | được khuyến khích làm gì | On Monday, residents are encouraged to walk along the trails and take photographs. |

## Động từ + V-ing

| Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ trong đề |
|---|---|---|
| **do a good/great job (of) + V-ing** | Làm tốt/xuất sắc việc gì | Once the roofing was finished, the crew did a great job cleaning up. |
| **look forward to + V-ing** | mong chờ (được) làm gì | I look forward to hearing from you. |
| **prevent ... from + V-ing** | ngăn ai/cái gì khỏi làm gì | April: Pest Deterrence - Learn natural, pesticide-free techniques that can prevent harmful insects from eating your fruits and vegetables. |

## Liên từ cặp / so sánh

| Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ trong đề |
|---|---|---|
| **both ... and** | cả ... và ... | Buses on both the orange and the blue routes stop at the zoo entrance. |
| **as ... as** | bằng/như (so sánh ngang bằng) | We will get to it as soon as we can. |
| **as soon as** | ngay khi | We will get to it as soon as we can. |
| **despite / in spite of** | mặc dù (+ N/V-ing) | Sometimes I considered shutting it all down and going back to my former job." Despite the difficult start, Mr. Rosier persevered. |
| **whether ... or** | liệu ... hay ... | In your e-mail, you should also indicate whether you wish to trade in your bicycle or place it on consignment. |

## Mẫu câu khác

| Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ trong đề |
|---|---|---|
| **be supposed to** | được cho là nên/phải làm gì | The custodian is out today, and I was supposed to do it. |
| **make sure (that)** | đảm bảo rằng | I want to make sure that my estimates are sound. |
| **ensure that** | đảm bảo rằng | Finally, ensure that you have carefully proofread your statement before submitting it. |
| **take/welcome the opportunity to** | nắm lấy cơ hội để làm gì | As part of our plan, we would welcome the opportunity to form a partnership with you. |
