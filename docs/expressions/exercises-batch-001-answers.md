# Đáp án bài tập đợt 1

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-001.md)

## A. Dịch Việt → Anh (10)

1. **Could you look into this bug and get back to me?** — look into = điều tra vấn đề; không dùng look up.
2. **We have to push back the release to Monday. / We have to push the release back to Monday.** — push back = dời muộn hơn; tách được.
3. **I just found out (that) she's moving.** — Nhận được thông tin → find out.
4. **I can't figure out why this test only fails on Firefox.** — Phải tự suy luận → figure out + why-clause.
5. **They called off the meeting because the boss was sick.** — Hủy hẳn → call off.
6. **Before we start, let's make sure we're on the same page about the scope.** — be on the same page (about).
7. **I take full responsibility for this mistake.** — take responsibility FOR.
8. **It took us three days to track down the memory leak.** — track down = truy tìm đến cùng.
9. **That's easier said than done.** — Thành ngữ cố định.
10. **As far as I know, the store is open until ten.** — Rào đón thông tin không chắc 100%.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **up** — set up an environment.
2. **off** — put off + V-ing/N = trì hoãn.
3. **the (address the issue — không có giới từ)** — address là ngoại động từ, không cần to.
4. **about** — raise concerns about/over.
5. **head** — Thành ngữ cố định.
6. **with** — cope with (không có up).
7. **one** — back to square one.
8. **down** — narrow down (to).
9. **out** — it turned out (that).
10. **rolling** — get the ball rolling (V-ing).

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **take over** — Tiếp quản việc người khác đang làm.
2. **ran into** — Gặp NGƯỜI tình cờ → run into tự nhiên hơn.
3. **gave in** — Nhượng bộ trước áp lực.
4. **look it up** — Tra từ; đại từ đứng giữa.
5. **carried out** — Thực hiện một hoạt động cụ thể.
6. **pointed out** — Chỉ ra một sự thật + that-clause.
7. **following up** — Hỏi lại tiến độ → follow up on.
8. **meet** — Collocation: meet a deadline.
9. **roll out** — Phát hành dần.
10. **go that far** — Phản đối nhẹ nhận định quá đà.

## D. Viết lại câu (5)

1. **We put off the demo until next week. / We pushed the demo back to next week.** — postpone = put off / push back.
2. **I'll look into the problem / look into it.** — investigate → look into.
3. **The meeting was called off.** — cancel → call off; bị động be called off.
4. **The budget is the elephant in the room.** — the elephant in the room.
5. **Losing my job turned out to be a blessing in disguise.** — a blessing in disguise + turn out to be.

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *We ran into… At first I couldn't figure out… It turned out that… In the end we sorted it out by…*
2. Gợi ý khung: *I'm guilty of putting off… Eventually I bite the bullet and… Stopping procrastination is easier said than done.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *Someone brought up… X pointed out that… After a long discussion we reached a consensus… so everyone was on the same page.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *We were back to square one… but it was a blessing in disguise because… At the end of the day…*
5. Gợi ý khung: *Some employees are happy to go the extra mile… That being said, not everyone can cope with… Companies shouldn't expect staff to take on…*
