## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **size up** — PRO-PHR-0141 · size sb/sth up (T-S).
2. **rope in** — PRO-PHR-0135 · rope sb in (to + V) (T-S).
3. **pass over (for)** — PRO-PHR-0128 · be passed over for promotion.
4. **screen out** — PRO-PHR-0137 · screen sb/sth out (T-S).
5. **top up** — PRO-PHR-0149 · top sth up (T-S); danh từ: top-up.
6. **pitch in** — PRO-PHR-0129 · pitch in (with sth).
7. **ease off** — PRO-PHR-0161 · ease off (on sth).
8. **run with** — PRO-PHR-0136 · run with sth.
9. **play out** — PRO-PHR-0130 · sth plays out (I).
10. **stock up (on)** — PRO-PHR-0146 · stock up (on sth).

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **up** — PRO-PHR-0141 · size up — đánh giá (tình hình, người)
2. **in** — PRO-PHR-0135 · rope in — lôi kéo (ai) giúp
3. **over** — PRO-PHR-0128 · pass over (for) — bỏ qua (khi thăng chức)
4. **out** — PRO-PHR-0137 · screen out — sàng lọc loại ra
5. **up** — PRO-PHR-0149 · top up — nạp thêm
6. **in** — PRO-PHR-0129 · pitch in — góp sức
7. **off** — PRO-PHR-0161 · ease off — giảm bớt
8. **with** — PRO-PHR-0136 · run with — theo đuổi (ý tưởng)
9. **out** — PRO-PHR-0130 · play out — diễn ra
10. **up** — PRO-PHR-0146 · stock up (on) — tích trữ

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **trim down** — trim down — cắt gọn (câu gốc: Let's trim down the test suite.)
2. **mock up** — mock up — làm bản mẫu (câu gốc: Can you mock up the new screen?)
3. **shore up** — shore up — củng cố (câu gốc: We shored up test coverage before the audit.)
4. **polish up** — polish up — trau chuốt (câu gốc: Let's polish up the report before sending.)
5. **max out** — max out — dùng tối đa (câu gốc: The CPU maxed out during the test.)
6. **stack up (against)** — stack up (against) — so sánh (với) (câu gốc: How does Playwright stack up against Cypress?)
7. **play along** — play along — giả vờ hợp tác (câu gốc: Just play along with the joke.)
8. **fizzle out** — fizzle out — xẹp dần, thất bại dần (câu gốc: The initiative fizzled out after a month.)
9. **lag behind** — lag behind — tụt hậu (câu gốc: Our test coverage lags behind the code.)
10. **gloss over** — gloss over — lướt qua, che lấp (câu gốc: Don't gloss over the risks in the report.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **Let's trim down the test suite.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). trim sth down (T-S).
2. **Can you mock up the new screen?** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). mock sth up (T-S); danh từ: mock-up.
3. **We shored up test coverage before the audit.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). shore up sth (T-S).
4. **Let's polish up the report before sending.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). polish sth up (T-S).
5. **The CPU maxed out during the test.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). max sth out (T-S); max out (I).
