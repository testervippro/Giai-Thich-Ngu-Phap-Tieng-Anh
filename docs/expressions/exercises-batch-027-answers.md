## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **it** — FIX-0042 · before I knew it — chưa kịp nhận ra thì
2. **same** — FIX-0027 · all the same — dù sao đi nữa
3. **so** — FIX-0026 · even so — dù vậy
4. **wrong** — FIX-0047 · correct me if I'm wrong — sai thì sửa giúp tôi
5. **point** — FIX-0067 · that's not the point — đó không phải là vấn đề
6. **it** — FIX-0044 · now that you mention it — bạn nhắc mới nhớ
7. **taken** — FIX-0059 · point taken — tôi hiểu ý bạn rồi
8. **it** — FIX-0056 · tell me about it — ai mà chẳng biết / tôi hiểu
9. **enough** — FIX-0058 · fair enough — cũng được, hợp lý
10. **words** — FIX-0023 · in other words — nói cách khác

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **at the same time** — at the same time — đồng thời; nhưng mà (câu gốc: At the same time, we can't ignore security.)
2. **if I'm not mistaken** — if I'm not mistaken — nếu tôi không nhầm (câu gốc: If I'm not mistaken, that endpoint is deprecated.)
3. **then again** — then again — nhưng mặt khác (câu gốc: It's risky. Then again, so is waiting.)
4. **in the end** — in the end — cuối cùng (câu gốc: In the end, we rolled back.)
5. **I reckon** — I reckon — tôi đoán là (câu gốc: I reckon it's a config issue.)
6. **after all** — after all — dù sao thì; rốt cuộc (câu gốc: Let's help them; they're our client, after all.)
7. **I'm inclined to think** — I'm inclined to think — tôi nghiêng về ý (câu gốc: I'm inclined to think it's the network.)
8. **I mean** — I mean — ý tôi là (câu gốc: It's slow — I mean, really slow.)
9. **you can say that again** — you can say that again — chuẩn luôn (câu gốc: “It's hot.” “You can say that again!”)
10. **on top of that** — on top of that — thêm vào đó (câu gốc: On top of that, the docs are outdated.)

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *Thanks all the same. / It rained. Even so, we had fun. / In other words, you're broke.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *Before I knew it, it was midnight. / Now that you mention it, she did look upset. / In the end, we took a taxi.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *Whether it's cheap is beside the point. / That's not the point. You lied. / With all due respect, you're wrong.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *Correct me if I'm wrong, but isn't it your turn? / If I'm not mistaken, we've met before. / I'm kind of tired.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *Point taken — I'll be on time. / “Mondays are awful.” “Tell me about it.” / “I'm too tired.” “Fair enough.”*
