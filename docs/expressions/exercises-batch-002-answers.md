# Đáp án bài tập đợt 2 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-002.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **get to the bottom of** — IDIOM-0037 · get to the bottom of + N.
2. **up to speed** — IDIOM-0046 · get/bring sb up to speed (on sth).
3. **take sth with a grain of salt** — IDIOM-0062 · take sth with a grain of salt (Mỹ) / a pinch of salt (Anh).
4. **touch base** — IDIOM-0048 · touch base (with sb) (on sth).
5. **make ends meet** — IDIOM-0019 · make ends meet; thường với struggle to / barely.
6. **my two cents** — IDIOM-0028 · That's my two cents; put/throw in my two cents.
7. **in the same boat** — IDIOM-0017 · be in the same boat (as sb).
8. **back to the drawing board** — IDIOM-0035 · It's back to the drawing board.
9. **spill the beans** — IDIOM-0022 · spill the beans (about sth).
10. **cross that bridge when we come to it** — IDIOM-0042 · We'll cross that bridge when we come to it.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **of** — IDIOM-0037 · get to the bottom of — tìm ra gốc rễ
2. **speed** — IDIOM-0046 · up to speed — nắm kịp thông tin
3. **salt** — IDIOM-0062 · take sth with a grain of salt — nghe nửa tin nửa ngờ
4. **base** — IDIOM-0048 · touch base — liên lạc, trao đổi nhanh
5. **meet** — IDIOM-0019 · make ends meet — xoay xở đủ sống
6. **cents** — IDIOM-0028 · my two cents — ý kiến nhỏ của tôi
7. **boat** — IDIOM-0017 · in the same boat — cùng cảnh ngộ
8. **board** — IDIOM-0035 · back to the drawing board — làm lại kế hoạch từ đầu
9. **beans** — IDIOM-0022 · spill the beans — lộ bí mật
10. **it** — IDIOM-0042 · cross that bridge when we come to it — tới đâu hay tới đó

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **in the loop** — in the loop — nắm được thông tin, được cập nhật (câu gốc: Please keep QA in the loop on any API changes.)
2. **by the book** — by the book — đúng quy định, theo sách (câu gốc: For audits, we have to do everything by the book.)
3. **cut to the chase** — cut to the chase — vào thẳng vấn đề (câu gốc: I'll cut to the chase: we're two weeks behind.)
4. **on the back burner** — on the back burner — tạm gác lại (câu gốc: The UI tests are on the back burner until the API is stable.)
5. **a quick fix** — a quick fix — giải pháp tạm thời, chữa cháy (câu gốc: This patch is a quick fix; we still need a proper solution.)
6. **cost an arm and a leg** — cost an arm and a leg — đắt cắt cổ (câu gốc: Enterprise licenses cost an arm and a leg.)
7. **hang in there** — hang in there — cố lên, ráng chịu (câu gốc: Hang in there, the release is almost done.)
8. **see eye to eye** — see eye to eye — đồng quan điểm (câu gốc: Dev and QA don't always see eye to eye on severity.)
9. **the bottom line** — the bottom line — điểm mấu chốt; lợi nhuận (câu gốc: Downtime directly hurts the bottom line.)
10. **from scratch** — from scratch — từ con số không (câu gốc: We rebuilt the framework from scratch.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **Please keep QA in the loop on any API changes.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). keep sb in the loop; be in/out of the loop.
2. **For audits, we have to do everything by the book.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). do sth / go by the book.
3. **I'll cut to the chase: we're two weeks behind.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). Let's cut to the chase.
4. **The UI tests are on the back burner until the API is stable.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). put sth on the back burner.
5. **This patch is a quick fix; we still need a proper solution.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). a quick fix (for sth); thường mang ý chê nhẹ.

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *Can you bring me up to speed on the gossip? / Let's touch base after the holidays. / Keep me in the loop about the party.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *With two kids, it's hard to make ends meet. / Don't worry, we're all in the same boat. / Come on, spill the beans! Who is she?*
3. Gợi ý khung: *I'm going to get to the bottom of this. / The recipe failed, so it's back to the drawing board. / What if it rains? We'll cross that bridge when we come to it.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *Just my two cents, but I'd buy the blue one. / Let's cut to the chase — how much do you want? / I've apologized. The ball is in her court now.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *Take his reviews with a grain of salt. / My dad and I don't see eye to eye on politics. / Your talk gave me a lot of food for thought.*
