## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **down to earth** — down to earth — giản dị, thực tế (câu gốc: Our CEO is surprisingly down-to-earth.)
2. **a breath of fresh air** — a breath of fresh air — làn gió mới (câu gốc: The new CTO is a breath of fresh air.)
3. **burn bridges** — burn bridges — cắt đứt quan hệ (không thể quay lại) (câu gốc: Leave on good terms — don't burn bridges.)
4. **bend over backwards** — bend over backwards — cố hết sức (để giúp) (câu gốc: We bent over backwards for that client.)
5. **call it quits** — call it quits — bỏ cuộc, dừng lại (câu gốc: Let's call it quits for today.)
6. **off the beaten track** — off the beaten track — nơi ít người lui tới (câu gốc: We found a village off the beaten track.)
7. **the best of both worlds** — the best of both worlds — vẹn cả đôi đường (câu gốc: Hybrid work gives us the best of both worlds.)
8. **in hot water** — in hot water — gặp rắc rối (câu gốc: The company is in hot water over data leaks.)
9. **break the bank** — break the bank — tốn quá nhiều tiền (câu gốc: This tool won't break the bank.)
10. **every cloud has a silver lining** — every cloud has a silver lining — trong cái rủi có cái may (câu gốc: You lost the job, but every cloud has a silver lining.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **My legs hurt, but no pain, no gain.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). Câu cố định.
2. **The new CTO is a breath of fresh air.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). be a breath of fresh air.
3. **Leave on good terms — don't burn bridges.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). burn one's bridges.
4. **We bent over backwards for that client.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). bend over backwards (to + V / for sb).
5. **Let's call it quits for today.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). call it quits.
