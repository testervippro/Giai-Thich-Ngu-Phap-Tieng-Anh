2. **have a lot on your plate** — IDIOM-0112 · have a lot / too much on one's plate.
3. **play it by ear** — IDIOM-0074 · play it by ear.
4. **make or break** — IDIOM-0080 · a make-or-break moment/year; sth can make or break sb.
5. **jump on the bandwagon** — IDIOM-0066 · jump/get on the bandwagon.
6. **with flying colors** — IDIOM-0085 · pass (sth) with flying colors (Anh: colours).
7. **hit the jackpot** — IDIOM-0082 · hit the jackpot.
8. **walk on eggshells** — IDIOM-0109 · walk on eggshells (around sb).
9. **go with the flow** — IDIOM-0073 · go with the flow.
10. **weigh the pros and cons** — IDIOM-0069 · weigh (up) the pros and cons (of sth).

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **through** — IDIOM-0088 · fall through — đổ bể, không thành
2. **ear** — IDIOM-0074 · play it by ear — tùy cơ ứng biến
3. **bandwagon** — IDIOM-0066 · jump on the bandwagon — đua theo trào lưu
4. **colors** — IDIOM-0085 · with flying colors — (đỗ) xuất sắc
5. **jackpot** — IDIOM-0082 · hit the jackpot — trúng lớn, gặp may lớn
6. **eggshells** — IDIOM-0109 · walk on eggshells — đi trên vỏ trứng (rất dè chừng)
7. **flow** — IDIOM-0073 · go with the flow — thuận theo tự nhiên
8. **cons** — IDIOM-0069 · weigh the pros and cons — cân nhắc ưu nhược
9. **doubt** — IDIOM-0108 · give sb the benefit of the doubt — tin tưởng ai dù chưa chắc
10. **boat** — IDIOM-0089 · miss the boat — lỡ cơ hội

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **make or break** — make or break — quyết định thành bại (câu gốc: The demo is make-or-break for the deal.)
2. **go down the drain** — go down the drain — đổ sông đổ biển (câu gốc: Months of work went down the drain when the project was cancelled.)
3. **get cold feet** — get cold feet — chùn bước, lo sợ phút chót (câu gốc: The client got cold feet about the migration.)
4. **by the skin of my teeth** — by the skin of my teeth — suýt soát, sát nút (câu gốc: I passed the exam by the skin of my teeth.)
5. **a tough call** — a tough call — quyết định khó (câu gốc: Delaying the release was a tough call.)
6. **the other side of the coin** — the other side of the coin — mặt kia của vấn đề (câu gốc: Speed is great; the other side of the coin is stability.)
7. **pay off** — pay off — được đền đáp (câu gốc: Investing in test automation paid off.)
8. **put all your eggs in one basket** — put all your eggs in one basket — bỏ hết trứng vào một giỏ (câu gốc: Don't put all your eggs in one basket — apply to several schools.)
9. **hit it off** — hit it off — hợp nhau ngay (câu gốc: The new hire hit it off with the team.)
10. **over the moon** — over the moon — vui sướng tột cùng (câu gốc: The client was over the moon with the demo.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **Let's give the vendor the benefit of the doubt this time.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). give sb the benefit of the doubt.
2. **We missed the boat on mobile.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). miss the boat.
3. **Months of work went down the drain when the project was cancelled.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). sth goes down the drain.
4. **I need to get this off my chest.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). get sth off one's chest.
5. **The client got cold feet about the migration.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). get cold feet (about sth).

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *I'm walking on eggshells around my boss. / I'll give him the benefit of the doubt. / I need to get this off my chest.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *Our plans fell through at the last minute. / This interview is make or break for me. / She passed her driving test with flying colors.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *Let's play it by ear and see how we feel. / I don't plan trips — I just go with the flow. / I'm weighing the pros and cons of moving.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *Everyone's jumping on the TikTok bandwagon. / It's well paid. The other side of the coin is the long hours. / Fame is a double-edged sword.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *I have a lot on my plate this week.*
