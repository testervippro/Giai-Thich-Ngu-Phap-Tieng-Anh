# Đáp án bài tập đợt 23 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-023.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **waste time** — COL-0062 · waste time (on sth / V-ing).
2. **have an impact on** — COL-0046 · have an impact on + N; a significant/huge impact.
3. **gain popularity** — COL-0066 · gain popularity (among sb).
4. **play a part** — COL-0075 · play a part in + N.
5. **give permission** — COL-0043 · give (sb) permission (to + V).
6. **take a look (at)** — COL-0039 · take / have a look (at sth).
7. **resolve an issue** — COL-0072 · resolve an issue/conflict/dispute.
8. **meet requirements** — COL-0084 · meet / satisfy requirements/standards.
9. **solve a problem** — COL-0070 · solve / tackle / resolve a problem.
10. **take a seat** — COL-0040 · Please take a seat.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **time** — COL-0062 · waste time — lãng phí thời gian
2. **popularity** — COL-0066 · gain popularity — trở nên phổ biến
3. **part** — COL-0075 · play a part — góp phần
4. **look** — COL-0039 · take a look (at) — xem qua
5. **seat** — COL-0040 · take a seat — ngồi xuống
6. **awareness** — COL-0077 · raise awareness — nâng cao nhận thức
7. **to** — COL-0045 · give priority to — ưu tiên
8. **time** — COL-0061 · save time — tiết kiệm thời gian
9. **decision** — COL-0081 · reach a decision — đi đến quyết định
10. **agreement** — COL-0080 · reach an agreement — đạt thỏa thuận

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **meet the needs** — meet the needs — đáp ứng nhu cầu (câu gốc: The hotel meets the needs of families.)
2. **raise money** — raise money — quyên tiền (câu gốc: We raised money for the hospital.)
3. **pose a threat** — pose a threat — gây mối đe dọa (câu gốc: Smoking poses a threat to your health.)
4. **pose a challenge** — pose a challenge — đặt ra thách thức (câu gốc: Flaky networks pose a challenge for e2e tests.)
5. **run a business** — run a business — điều hành công việc kinh doanh (câu gốc: Running a business requires discipline.)
6. **set a goal** — set a goal — đặt mục tiêu (câu gốc: We set a goal of 80% coverage.)
7. **have fun** — have fun — vui vẻ (câu gốc: Have fun at the party!)
8. **catch a cold** — catch a cold — bị cảm (câu gốc: I think I'm catching a cold.)
9. **draw attention to** — draw attention to — thu hút sự chú ý đến (câu gốc: I want to draw attention to the risk.)
10. **take the lead** — take the lead — dẫn đầu; chủ động (câu gốc: Lan will take the lead on test planning.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **QA plays a key role in release decisions.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). play a (key/vital/major) role in + N/V-ing.
2. **We ran a workshop to raise awareness of security testing.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). raise awareness of/about + N.
3. **Give priority to the payment bugs.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). give priority to + N.
4. **Automation saves us hours every week.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). save time / money / effort.
5. **The board will reach a decision Monday.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). reach / come to a decision.

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *Don't waste time on social media. / Sleep has a big impact on mood. / The game is gaining popularity.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *Luck played a part in our success. / My parents gave me permission to go. / Take a look at this!*
3. Gợi ý khung: *They resolved their differences. / Does this flat meet your requirements? / Let's solve this problem together.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *Please take a seat. / Diet plays a big role in health. / The event raised awareness of cancer.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *We give priority to older people. / Taking the highway saves time. / We haven't reached a decision yet.*
