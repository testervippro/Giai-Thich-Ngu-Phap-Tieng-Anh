# Đáp án bài tập đợt 11 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-011.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **hear from** — PHR-0146 · hear from sb.
2. **go over** — PHR-0132 · go over + N (không tách).
3. **head out** — PHR-0145 · head out (I).
4. **hang up** — PHR-0143 · hang up (on sb) (I); hang sth up (T-S).
5. **look back (on)** — PHR-0166 · look back (on sth).
6. **let up** — PHR-0159 · sth lets up (I).
7. **make up for** — PHR-0175 · make up for + N.
8. **grow up** — PHR-0138 · grow up (in a place); Grow up!
9. **go with** — PHR-0136 · go with + N.
10. **go out** — PHR-0131 · go out (with sb); the lights went out.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **from** — PHR-0146 · hear from — nhận tin từ
2. **over** — PHR-0132 · go over — xem lại, rà soát
3. **up** — PHR-0143 · hang up — cúp máy; treo lên
4. **back** — PHR-0166 · look back (on) — nhìn lại (quá khứ)
5. **up** — PHR-0159 · let up — ngớt, giảm
6. **for** — PHR-0175 · make up for — bù đắp
7. **up** — PHR-0138 · grow up — lớn lên; người lớn lên đi
8. **with** — PHR-0136 · go with — hợp với; chọn
9. **out** — PHR-0131 · go out — đi chơi; tắt (đèn); hẹn hò
10. **on** — PHR-0162 · live on — sống bằng (tiền/thức ăn)

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **hang on** — hang on — đợi chút; bám chặt (câu gốc: Hang on — the tests are still running.)
2. **keep up (with)** — keep up (with) — theo kịp; duy trì (câu gốc: Keep up the good work, team!)
3. **hold on** — hold on — chờ; giữ chặt (câu gốc: Hold on, I'm seeing a different error.)
4. **look up to** — look up to — kính trọng, ngưỡng mộ (câu gốc: Junior testers look up to her.)
5. **hold up** — hold up — làm chậm; cướp; trụ vững (câu gốc: Code review is holding up the release.)
6. **go up** — go up — tăng (câu gốc: Response time went up after the change.)
7. **look down on** — look down on — coi thường (câu gốc: Don't look down on people who earn less.)
8. **lay off** — lay off — sa thải (vì thiếu việc); thôi đi (câu gốc: The company laid off 10% of staff.)
9. **go off** — go off — (chuông) reo; nổ; (đồ ăn) hỏng (câu gốc: The monitoring alert went off at 3 a.m.)
10. **look out** — look out — coi chừng (câu gốc: Look out for memory leaks in this module.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **Students live on instant noodles.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). live on + N.
2. **Hang on — the tests are still running.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). Hang on (a second/minute); hang on to sth.
3. **Keep up the good work, team!** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). keep up (with sb/sth); keep up the good work.
4. **Hold on, I'm seeing a different error.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). Hold on (a second); hold on to sth.
5. **Junior testers look up to her.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). look up to sb.

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *Have you heard from Tom? / Let's go over the plan again. / I'm heading out — see you tomorrow.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *Don't hang up on me! / Looking back, it was the best year of my life. / The rain finally let up.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *I'll make up for missing your birthday. / I grew up in Hue. / Does this tie go with my shirt?*
4. Gợi ý khung: *Let's go out tonight. / Students live on instant noodles. / Hang on, I'll be right back.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *It's hard to keep up with technology. / Hold on, let me get a pen. / I've always looked up to my dad.*
