# Đáp án bài tập đợt 30 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-030.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **to put it another way** — FIX-0187 · To put it another way, …
2. **truth be told** — FIX-0191 · Truth be told, …
3. **a hectic schedule** — COL-0244 · a hectic / busy / packed schedule.
4. **provide an opportunity** — COL-0236 · provide / offer an opportunity (for sb to + V).
5. **and so on** — FIX-0176 · …, and so on (and so forth).
6. **bear in mind** — COL-0228 · bear / keep in mind (that)…
7. **run out of patience** — COL-0237 · run out of patience / lose patience (with sb).
8. **place emphasis on** — COL-0235 · place / put emphasis on + N.
9. **let's face it** — FIX-0192 · Let's face it, …
10. **not necessarily** — FIX-0183 · Not necessarily.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **way** — FIX-0187 · to put it another way — nói cách khác
2. **told** — FIX-0191 · truth be told — nói thật thì
3. **schedule** — COL-0244 · a hectic schedule — lịch kín mít
4. **opportunity** — COL-0236 · provide an opportunity — tạo cơ hội
5. **on** — FIX-0176 · and so on — vân vân
6. **mind** — COL-0228 · bear in mind — ghi nhớ, lưu ý
7. **patience** — COL-0237 · run out of patience — hết kiên nhẫn
8. **on** — COL-0235 · place emphasis on — nhấn mạnh, chú trọng
9. **it** — FIX-0192 · let's face it — hãy đối diện sự thật
10. **necessarily** — FIX-0183 · not necessarily — chưa chắc

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **work overtime** — work overtime — làm thêm giờ (câu gốc: We worked overtime before the launch.)
2. **a competitive salary** — a competitive salary — mức lương cạnh tranh (câu gốc: We offer a competitive salary.)
3. **draw up a plan** — draw up a plan — lập kế hoạch (câu gốc: We drew up a plan for the trip.)
4. **an integral part** — an integral part — phần không thể thiếu (câu gốc: Testing is an integral part of development.)
5. **fill a gap** — fill a gap — lấp chỗ trống (câu gốc: This tool fills a gap in our test stack.)
6. **just to clarify** — just to clarify — làm rõ chút (câu gốc: Just to clarify, is mobile in scope?)
7. **I see what you mean** — I see what you mean — tôi hiểu ý bạn (câu gốc: I see what you mean about the flaky test.)
8. **that kind of thing** — that kind of thing — mấy thứ kiểu vậy (câu gốc: I like hiking, camping, that kind of thing.)
9. **I'm all ears** — I'm all ears — tôi đang nghe đây (câu gốc: Got a better idea? I'm all ears.)
10. **as I was saying** — as I was saying — như tôi đang nói (câu gốc: As I was saying, the API is slow.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **We worked overtime before the launch.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). work overtime / long hours.
2. **We offer a competitive salary.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). a competitive salary/edge.
3. **Let's draw up a test plan.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). draw up / devise / come up with a plan.
4. **Testing is an integral part of development.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). an integral part of + N.
5. **This tool fills a gap in our test stack.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). fill a gap (in the market).

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *The trip provided an opportunity to relax. / Bear in mind that the shop closes early. / I'm running out of patience with him.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *I have a hectic schedule this week. / - / Rice is an integral part of our diet.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *We bought bread, milk, eggs and so on. / I like hiking, camping, that kind of thing. / Off the top of my head, about fifty.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *Truth be told, I was scared. / Let's face it, nobody likes Mondays. / Anyway, as I was saying…*
5. Gợi ý khung: *To put it another way, it's too expensive. / Just to clarify, are we meeting at six? / Let me get this straight — you sold the car?*
