# Đáp án bài tập đợt 8 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-008.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **back out (of)** — PHR-0021 · back out (of + N/V-ing).
2. **a grey area** — IDIOM-0317 · be a grey (Mỹ: gray) area.
3. **an acquired taste** — IDIOM-0323 · be an acquired taste.
4. **leave a lot to be desired** — IDIOM-0333 · sth leaves a lot to be desired.
5. **spread yourself too thin** — IDIOM-0351 · spread oneself too thin.
6. **in hindsight** — IDIOM-0331 · In hindsight, …
7. **see red** — IDIOM-0340 · see red.
8. **break down** — PHR-0026 · break down (I); break sth down (into parts) (T-S); danh từ: breakdown.
9. **have the upper hand** — IDIOM-0330 · have / gain the upper hand.
10. **back to the grind** — IDIOM-0324 · back to the grind.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **area** — IDIOM-0317 · a grey area — vùng xám (không rõ ràng)
2. **taste** — IDIOM-0323 · an acquired taste — thứ phải tập mới thích
3. **desired** — IDIOM-0333 · leave a lot to be desired — còn nhiều điều chưa ổn
4. **thin** — IDIOM-0351 · spread yourself too thin — ôm đồm quá nhiều
5. **hindsight** — IDIOM-0331 · in hindsight — nhìn lại thì
6. **red** — IDIOM-0340 · see red — nổi giận
7. **down** — PHR-0026 · break down — hỏng; suy sụp; phân tích nhỏ
8. **hand** — IDIOM-0330 · have the upper hand — chiếm thế thượng phong
9. **grind** — IDIOM-0324 · back to the grind — quay lại guồng công việc
10. **point** — IDIOM-0319 · a moot point — vấn đề còn bàn cãi / không còn liên quan

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **break in** — break in — đột nhập; ngắt lời; làm quen (đồ mới) (câu gốc: Sorry to break in, but the client is on the line.)
2. **keep tabs on** — keep tabs on — theo dõi sát (câu gốc: We keep tabs on error rates.)
3. **cut no ice** — cut no ice — chẳng có tác dụng (câu gốc: His excuses cut no ice with me.)
4. **a game changer** — a game changer — nhân tố thay đổi cuộc chơi (câu gốc: Parallel testing was a game changer for us.)
5. **the writing is on the wall** — the writing is on the wall — điềm báo rõ ràng (câu gốc: The writing is on the wall for the old framework.)
6. **a bone of contention** — a bone of contention — nguồn gốc tranh cãi (câu gốc: Test ownership is a bone of contention.)
7. **act up** — act up — trục trặc; quậy, hư (trẻ con) (câu gốc: The test runner is acting up again on Windows.)
8. **go against the grain** — go against the grain — đi ngược số đông (câu gốc: It goes against the grain for me to waste food.)
9. **back down** — back down — nhượng bộ, lùi bước (câu gốc: Governments sometimes back down under public pressure.)
10. **over the hill** — over the hill — quá thời đỉnh cao (già) (câu gốc: At 40, I'm hardly over the hill!)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **His story sounds far-fetched.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). far-fetched (idea/story).
2. **Whether he'd agree is a moot point — he's gone.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). be a moot point.
3. **Sorry to break in, but the client is on the line.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). break in (I); break into + N (T-I).
4. **We keep tabs on error rates.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). keep tabs on + N.
5. **His excuses cut no ice with me.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). cut no ice (with sb).

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *Using AI for homework is a grey area. / Durian is an acquired taste. / The service left a lot to be desired.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *He backed out at the last minute. / My car broke down on the highway. / Someone broke in while we were away.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *Don't spread yourself too thin. / In hindsight, I should have studied harder. / When he insulted my mom, I saw red.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *The home team had the upper hand. / Holiday's over — back to the grind. / His story sounds far-fetched.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *Whether he'd agree is a moot point — he's gone. / My mom keeps tabs on my spending. / His excuses cut no ice with me.*
