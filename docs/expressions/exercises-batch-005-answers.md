## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **once bitten, twice shy** — once bitten, twice shy — chim phải đạn sợ cành cong (câu gốc: I won't lend him money again — once bitten, twice shy.)
2. **your guess is as good as mine** — your guess is as good as mine — tôi cũng không biết hơn bạn (câu gốc: When will it stop raining? Your guess is as good as mine.)
3. **behind the scenes** — behind the scenes — hậu trường (câu gốc: QA does a lot of work behind the scenes.)
4. **save for a rainy day** — save for a rainy day — tiết kiệm phòng khi khó khăn (câu gốc: People should save for a rainy day.)
5. **take it easy** — take it easy — bình tĩnh; nghỉ ngơi (câu gốc: Take it easy — you've been working too hard.)
6. **not my cup of tea** — not my cup of tea — không hợp gu tôi (câu gốc: Opera's not my cup of tea.)
7. **on thin ice** — on thin ice — ở thế nguy hiểm (câu gốc: The project is on thin ice with the client.)
8. **make a mountain out of a molehill** — make a mountain out of a molehill — chuyện bé xé ra to (câu gốc: It's a typo; let's not make a mountain out of a molehill.)
9. **on the right track** — on the right track — đi đúng hướng (câu gốc: We're on the right track with this fix.)
10. **it's a small world** — it's a small world — trái đất tròn (câu gốc: You know my cousin? It's a small world!)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **I won't lend him money again — once bitten, twice shy.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). Once bitten, twice shy.
2. **When will it stop raining? Your guess is as good as mine.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). Your guess is as good as mine.
3. **QA does a lot of work behind the scenes.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). behind the scenes; behind-the-scenes work.
4. **I save a little for a rainy day.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). save (money) for a rainy day.
5. **It's raining cats and dogs out there!** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). It's raining cats and dogs.
