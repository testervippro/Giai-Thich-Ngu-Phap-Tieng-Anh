# Đáp án bài tập đợt 9 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-009.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **cut out** — PHR-0073 · cut sth out (T-S); Cut it out! (thôi đi); be cut out for (hợp với).
2. **come to** — PHR-0064 · come to (I); come to + số tiền.
3. **close down** — PHR-0054 · close down (I); close sth down (T-S).
4. **call on** — PHR-0042 · call on sb to + V.
5. **cut down (on)** — PHR-0070 · cut down (on + N).
6. **clear up** — PHR-0053 · clear up sth (T-S); the weather clears up (I).
7. **catch up (with/on)** — PHR-0047 · catch up (with sb); catch up on sth (sleep, work).
8. **check in** — PHR-0048 · check in (at/to a hotel); check in with sb.
9. **come down with** — PHR-0059 · come down with + bệnh.
10. **check up on** — PHR-0050 · check up on sb/sth.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **out** — PHR-0073 · cut out — bỏ (thói quen); cắt ra
2. **to** — PHR-0064 · come to — tỉnh lại; tổng cộng là
3. **down** — PHR-0054 · close down — đóng cửa (vĩnh viễn)
4. **on** — PHR-0042 · call on — kêu gọi; gọi (ai) phát biểu; ghé thăm
5. **down** — PHR-0070 · cut down (on) — giảm bớt
6. **up** — PHR-0053 · clear up — làm rõ; (trời) quang; dọn
7. **up** — PHR-0047 · catch up (with/on) — bắt kịp; hàn huyên; làm bù
8. **in** — PHR-0048 · check in — làm thủ tục nhận phòng/bay; hỏi thăm
9. **with** — PHR-0059 · come down with — bị nhiễm (bệnh nhẹ)
10. **on** — PHR-0050 · check up on — kiểm tra (xem ổn không)

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **come out** — come out — ra mắt; lộ ra; công khai (câu gốc: The new iOS version comes out in September.)
2. **calm down** — calm down — bình tĩnh lại (câu gốc: Let's calm down and look at the logs.)
3. **cut off** — cut off — cắt (điện, nước); ngắt lời; cô lập (câu gốc: The VPN keeps cutting me off.)
4. **die down** — die down — lắng xuống (câu gốc: The hype around the tool died down.)
5. **crack up** — crack up — cười phá lên; suy sụp (câu gốc: His jokes crack me up.)
6. **bring on** — bring on — gây ra (bệnh, vấn đề) (câu gốc: The bug was brought on by a config change.)
7. **brush off** — brush off — gạt đi, phớt lờ (câu gốc: Don't brush off flaky tests as noise.)
8. **do away with** — do away with — bãi bỏ (câu gốc: Let's do away with manual deployments.)
9. **call back** — call back — gọi lại (câu gốc: I'll call the vendor back after lunch.)
10. **come over** — come over — ghé nhà chơi (câu gốc: Come over after work!)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **The new iOS version comes out in September.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). sth comes out (I); come out with sth.
2. **Let's calm down and look at the logs.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). calm down (I); calm sb down (T-S).
3. **The VPN keeps cutting me off.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). cut sth off (T-S); be cut off from.
4. **The hype around the tool died down.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). sth dies down.
5. **His jokes crack me up.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). crack up (I); crack sb up (T-S).

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *I've cut out sugar completely. / The bill came to 80 dollars. / My favorite café closed down.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *The teacher called on me first. / You need to cut down on junk food. / I hope it clears up later.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *Let's grab coffee and catch up. / We checked in at noon. / I think I'm coming down with a cold.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *I'm just checking up on you. / Her new album comes out next week. / Calm down and tell me what happened.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *Our internet was cut off. / Wait until the wind dies down. / His jokes crack me up.*
