# Bài tập đợt 1

## A. Dịch Việt → Anh (10)

1. Bạn có thể xem xét lỗi này rồi báo lại cho tôi không?
2. Chúng ta phải dời ngày phát hành sang thứ Hai.
3. Tôi vừa biết được là cô ấy sắp chuyển nhà.
4. Tôi không hiểu nổi tại sao bài test này chỉ lỗi trên Firefox.
5. Họ đã hủy cuộc họp vì sếp bị ốm.
6. Trước khi bắt đầu, hãy chắc rằng chúng ta thống nhất về phạm vi.
7. Tôi xin nhận toàn bộ trách nhiệm về lỗi này.
8. Mất ba ngày chúng tôi mới lần ra lỗi rò rỉ bộ nhớ.
9. Nói thì dễ, làm mới khó.
10. Theo tôi biết thì cửa hàng mở đến 10 giờ.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. I'll set ___ a test environment for you this afternoon.
2. Stop putting ___ your homework!
3. We need to address ___ issue of flaky tests. (điền mạo từ hoặc để trống nếu cần giới từ)
4. Several users raised concerns ___ the new privacy policy.
5. I think you've hit the nail on the ___.
6. How do you cope ___ so much pressure?
7. The fix didn't work, so we're back to square ___.
8. We've narrowed it ___ to two possible causes.
9. It turned ___ that the server clock was wrong.
10. Let's get the ball ___ on the new project.

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. Minh will ___ the project while I'm on maternity leave.  
   (take on / take over / take up)
2. I ___ my old classmate at the airport yesterday.  
   (came across / ran into / looked into)
3. The kids kept crying, so I finally ___.  
   (gave up / gave in / gave out)
4. I don't know this word. I'll ___.  
   (look it up / look into it / look up it)
5. We ___ load testing on the API last week.  
   (carried out / followed through / called off)
6. She ___ that the numbers didn't add up.  
   (brought up / pointed out / found out)
7. I'm just ___ on the email I sent on Monday.  
   (following through / following up / following out)
8. I couldn't ___ the deadline because my laptop died.  
   (catch / reach / meet)
9. We'll ___ the feature to 5% of users first.  
   (roll out / roll back / spin up)
10. “This is the best phone ever made.” “I wouldn't ___.”  
   (go that far / go too long / go further)

## D. Viết lại câu (5)

1. We postponed the demo until next week. (dùng phrasal verb)
2. I'll investigate the problem. (thân mật hơn)
3. The meeting was cancelled. (phrasal verb, bị động)
4. Nobody wants to talk about the real problem: the budget. (idiom)
5. Losing my job seemed terrible, but it led to something better. (idiom)

## E. Nói (5)

1. Describe a time you had to deal with a difficult problem at work or school. Dùng: run into, figure out, sort out, it turned out that.
2. Do you tend to put things off? Why? Dùng: put off, bite the bullet, easier said than done.
3. Talk about a decision your team made together. Dùng: be on the same page, reach a consensus, bring up, point out.
4. Describe a time something bad turned out well. Dùng: a blessing in disguise, back to square one, at the end of the day.
5. IELTS Part 3: Should companies make employees work overtime? Dùng: go the extra mile, cope with, take on, that being said.
