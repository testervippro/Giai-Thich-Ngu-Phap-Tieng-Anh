## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **no worries** — no worries — không sao đâu (câu gốc: No worries, I'll rerun it.)
2. **as for** — as for — còn về (câu gốc: As for the API, it's stable.)
3. **first and foremost** — first and foremost — trước hết và quan trọng nhất (câu gốc: First and foremost, we need tests.)
4. **you've got to be kidding me** — you've got to be kidding me — bạn đùa à (câu gốc: The build failed again? You've got to be kidding me.)
5. **believe me** — believe me — tin tôi đi (câu gốc: Believe me, it's worth automating.)
6. **the bottom line is** — the bottom line is — điều cốt yếu là (câu gốc: The bottom line is, it's not ready.)
7. **by and large** — by and large — nhìn chung (câu gốc: By and large, the release went well.)
8. **good to know** — good to know — biết vậy cũng tốt (câu gốc: Good to know — I'll update the docs.)
9. **my bad** — my bad — lỗi tôi (câu gốc: My bad — wrong branch.)
10. **are you with me?** — are you with me? — bạn theo kịp không? (câu gốc: First turn left, then right. Are you with me?)
