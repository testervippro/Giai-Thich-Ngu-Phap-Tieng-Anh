## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **going forward** — FIX-0135 · Going forward, …
2. **from time to time** — FIX-0120 · from time to time.
3. **I hate to interrupt, but** — FIX-0165 · Sorry to interrupt, but / I hate to interrupt, but …
4. **I'll keep you posted** — FIX-0131 · I'll keep you posted (on sth).
5. **what's the catch?** — FIX-0153 · What's the catch?
6. **it's up to you** — FIX-0129 · It's up to you.
7. **on second thought** — FIX-0150 · On second thought (Anh: thoughts), …
8. **in a way** — FIX-0157 · In a way, …
9. **I'll get back to you** — FIX-0132 · I'll get back to you (on sth).
10. **I'm on it** — FIX-0141 · I'm on it.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **forward** — FIX-0135 · going forward — từ giờ trở đi
2. **time** — FIX-0120 · from time to time — thỉnh thoảng
3. **but** — FIX-0165 · I hate to interrupt, but — xin lỗi đã ngắt lời, nhưng
4. **posted** — FIX-0131 · I'll keep you posted — tôi sẽ báo bạn
5. **catch** — FIX-0153 · what's the catch? — có vấn đề gì đằng sau?
6. **you** — FIX-0129 · it's up to you — tùy bạn
7. **thought** — FIX-0150 · on second thought — nghĩ lại thì
8. **way** — FIX-0157 · in a way — theo một cách nào đó
9. **you** — FIX-0132 · I'll get back to you — tôi sẽ trả lời bạn sau
10. **it** — FIX-0141 · I'm on it — tôi xử lý ngay

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **at the last minute** — at the last minute — vào phút chót (câu gốc: Requirements changed at the last minute.)
2. **better late than never** — better late than never — muộn còn hơn không (câu gốc: You finally called! Better late than never.)
3. **give or take** — give or take — xê dịch khoảng (câu gốc: It'll take two days, give or take.)
4. **that's all from me** — that's all from me — tôi chỉ có vậy thôi (câu gốc: That's all from me. Any questions?)
5. **any questions?** — any questions? — có câu hỏi không? (câu gốc: That's the plan. Any questions?)
6. **my hands are tied** — my hands are tied — tôi bất lực (không có quyền) (câu gốc: My hands are tied — it's the client's decision.)
7. **at this point** — at this point — vào lúc này (câu gốc: At this point, we can't change scope.)
8. **I hate to say this, but** — I hate to say this, but — tôi không muốn nói nhưng (câu gốc: I hate to say this, but we're not ready.)
9. **as soon as possible** — as soon as possible — càng sớm càng tốt (câu gốc: We need a fix as soon as possible.)
10. **more or less** — more or less — ít nhiều, gần như (câu gốc: We're more or less done.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **Worst-case scenario, we roll back.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). Worst-case scenario, … / In the worst-case scenario, …
2. **Requirements changed at the last minute.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). at the last minute; last-minute (tính từ).
3. **You finally called! Better late than never.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). Better late than never.
4. **It'll take two days, give or take.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). + số, give or take + số.
5. **That's all from me. Any questions?** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). That's all from me.

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *Going forward, I'll cook more. / I hate to interrupt, but dinner's ready. / No news yet, but I'll keep you posted.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *It's free? What's the catch? / Stay or go — it's up to you. / You finally called! Better late than never.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *In a way, losing was a good thing. / It's 50 kilometres, give or take. / I hate to say it, but I think he's right.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *I see him from time to time. / They cancelled at the last minute. / He's leaving Vietnam for good.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *On second thought, I'll have tea. / Worst-case scenario, we take the train. / Take an umbrella, just in case.*
