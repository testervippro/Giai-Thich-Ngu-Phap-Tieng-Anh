# Đáp án bài tập đợt 12 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-012.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **put forward** — PHR-0197 · put forward an idea/proposal (T-S).
2. **open up** — PHR-0181 · open up (to sb); open up opportunities.
3. **pay back** — PHR-0186 · pay sb back (T-S); danh từ: payback.
4. **shut down** — PHR-0216 · shut down (I); shut sth down (T-S); danh từ: shutdown.
5. **pick up** — PHR-0189 · pick sb/sth up (T-S); sth picks up (I).
6. **stand out** — PHR-0224 · stand out (from/among sth).
7. **pass away** — PHR-0182 · pass away (I).
8. **shut up** — PHR-0217 · Shut up! (thô); shut sb up (T-S).
9. **put up with** — PHR-0203 · put up with + N.
10. **pull off** — PHR-0191 · pull sth off (T-S).

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **forward** — PHR-0197 · put forward — đề xuất
2. **up** — PHR-0181 · open up — mở lòng; mở ra (cơ hội)
3. **back** — PHR-0186 · pay back — trả nợ; trả đũa
4. **down** — PHR-0216 · shut down — đóng cửa; tắt (máy)
5. **up** — PHR-0189 · pick up — đón; nhặt; học lỏm; tăng lên
6. **out** — PHR-0224 · stand out — nổi bật
7. **away** — PHR-0182 · pass away — qua đời
8. **up** — PHR-0217 · shut up — im đi (thô)
9. **with** — PHR-0203 · put up with — chịu đựng
10. **off** — PHR-0191 · pull off — làm được (việc khó)

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **put on** — put on — mặc vào; tăng (cân); bật (câu gốc: Put on your coat.)
2. **show off** — show off — khoe khoang (câu gốc: Let's show off the new dashboard to the client.)
3. **stand for** — stand for — viết tắt của; đại diện; chịu đựng (câu gốc: CI stands for continuous integration.)
4. **stand by** — stand by — ủng hộ; giữ lời; chờ sẵn (câu gốc: Please stand by for the deploy.)
5. **pass up** — pass up — bỏ lỡ (cơ hội) (câu gốc: I passed up the offer to stay in my team.)
6. **read up on** — read up on — đọc tìm hiểu kỹ (câu gốc: Read up on the API docs first.)
7. **sell out** — sell out — bán hết; bán rẻ lương tâm (câu gốc: The tickets sold out in an hour.)
8. **save up** — save up — tiết kiệm (để mua gì) (câu gốc: I'm saving up for a new laptop.)
9. **play down** — play down — giảm nhẹ (tầm quan trọng) (câu gốc: Don't play down the severity of the bug.)
10. **show up** — show up — xuất hiện, có mặt (câu gốc: The bug only shows up on mobile.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **Put on your coat.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). put sth on (T-S); put on weight.
2. **Let's show off the new dashboard to the client.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). show off (I); show sth off (T-S); danh từ: show-off.
3. **The police pulled me over.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). pull over (I); be pulled over (bị cảnh sát dừng).
4. **CI stands for continuous integration.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). stand for + N.
5. **Please stand by for the deploy.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). stand by sb/sth (T-I); stand by (I).

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *She put forward a good idea. / He finally opened up to me. / I'll pay you back next week.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *Shut down your laptop before you leave. / I'll pick you up at seven. / Her red dress really stood out.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *My grandfather passed away last year. / Oh, shut up! You're kidding! / I can't put up with the noise.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *I can't believe we pulled it off! / Put on your coat. / He's always showing off his car.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *The police pulled me over. / What does “QA” stand for? / I'll stand by you no matter what.*
