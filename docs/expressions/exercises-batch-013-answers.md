# Đáp án bài tập đợt 13 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-013.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **wipe out** — PHR-0260 · wipe sth out (T-S); be wiped out.
2. **turn out** — PHR-0252 · turn out (to be) + adj/N; turn out that…
3. **tell off** — PHR-0240 · tell sb off (for sth) (T-S).
4. **chip in** — PHR-0272 · chip in (for sth / with sth).
5. **tidy up** — PHR-0245 · tidy (sth) up.
6. **take down** — PHR-0231 · take sth down (T-S).
7. **watch out** — PHR-0256 · Watch out!; watch out for sth.
8. **turn down** — PHR-0248 · turn sb/sth down (T-S).
9. **apply for** — PHR-0264 · apply for + job/visa/scholarship.
10. **write down** — PHR-0261 · write sth down (T-S).

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **out** — PHR-0260 · wipe out — xóa sổ; làm kiệt sức
2. **out** — PHR-0252 · turn out — hóa ra; có mặt
3. **off** — PHR-0240 · tell off — mắng
4. **in** — PHR-0272 · chip in — góp tiền; góp lời
5. **up** — PHR-0245 · tidy up — dọn dẹp gọn
6. **down** — PHR-0231 · take down — ghi lại; gỡ xuống
7. **out** — PHR-0256 · watch out — coi chừng
8. **down** — PHR-0248 · turn down — từ chối; vặn nhỏ
9. **for** — PHR-0264 · apply for — nộp đơn xin
10. **down** — PHR-0261 · write down — ghi lại

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **cover up** — cover up — che đậy (câu gốc: Don't cover up failing tests with retries.)
2. **turn off** — turn off — tắt; làm mất hứng (câu gốc: Turn off notifications during the demo.)
3. **take out** — take out — lấy ra; dẫn đi chơi; vay (câu gốc: Let's take the debug logs out before release.)
4. **take off** — take off — cất cánh; cởi; nổi tiếng nhanh (câu gốc: The app really took off last year.)
5. **talk over** — talk over — bàn bạc (câu gốc: Let's talk this over with the team.)
6. **wear off** — wear off — hết tác dụng; phai dần (câu gốc: The novelty wore off after a week.)
7. **throw away** — throw away — vứt đi (câu gốc: Don't throw away the old logs yet.)
8. **take up** — take up — bắt đầu (sở thích); chiếm (chỗ/thời gian) (câu gốc: Meetings take up half my day.)
9. **boil down to** — boil down to — quy về, rốt cuộc là (câu gốc: The issue boils down to bad test data.)
10. **turn on** — turn on — bật (câu gốc: Turn on verbose logging.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **Don't cover up failing tests with retries.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). cover sth up (T-S); danh từ: cover-up.
2. **Turn off notifications during the demo.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). turn sth off (T-S); turn sb off.
3. **Let's take the debug logs out before release.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). take sb/sth out (T-S).
4. **The app really took off last year.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). take off (I); take sth off (T-S).
5. **Let's talk this over with the team.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). talk sth over (with sb) (T-S).

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *The flood wiped out the village. / The party turned out great. / The teacher told me off for being late.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *Let's all chip in for her gift. / Tidy up your desk before you leave. / Let me take down your number.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *Watch out! The floor is wet. / She turned down the job offer. / I'm applying for a visa.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *Write down my number. / They tried to cover up the scandal. / Turn off the lights when you leave.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *Can you take out the trash? / Take off your shoes, please. / Let's talk it over at dinner.*
