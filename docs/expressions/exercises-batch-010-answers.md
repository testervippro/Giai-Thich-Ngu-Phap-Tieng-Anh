# Đáp án bài tập đợt 10 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-010.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **get up** — PHR-0118 · get up (I); get sb up (T-S).
2. **figure on** — PHR-0094 · figure on + N/V-ing.
3. **get rid of** — PHR-0115 · get rid of + N.
4. **go ahead** — PHR-0123 · Go ahead (and + V); go ahead with sth.
5. **give back** — PHR-0120 · give sth back (T-S); give back to the community.
6. **get together** — PHR-0117 · get together; danh từ: a get-together.
7. **drag on** — PHR-0079 · sth drags on (I).
8. **get around** — PHR-0100 · get around (I); get around sth (T-I).
9. **give out** — PHR-0122 · give sth out (T-S); sth gives out (I).
10. **drop off** — PHR-0084 · drop sb/sth off (T-S); sth drops off (I).

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **up** — PHR-0118 · get up — thức dậy; đứng dậy
2. **on** — PHR-0094 · figure on — tính đến, dự tính (Mỹ)
3. **of** — PHR-0115 · get rid of — vứt bỏ, loại bỏ
4. **ahead** — PHR-0123 · go ahead — cứ làm đi; tiến hành
5. **back** — PHR-0120 · give back — trả lại; đóng góp cho cộng đồng
6. **together** — PHR-0117 · get together — tụ tập
7. **on** — PHR-0079 · drag on — kéo dài lê thê
8. **around** — PHR-0100 · get around — đi lại; lách (luật); lan truyền
9. **out** — PHR-0122 · give out — phát; hết, hỏng
10. **off** — PHR-0084 · drop off — đưa (ai) xuống xe; giảm; ngủ gật

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **fill out** — fill out — điền (đơn) (Mỹ) (câu gốc: Please fill out the bug report template.)
2. **drop out (of)** — drop out (of) — bỏ học; rút khỏi (câu gốc: Two people dropped out of the training.)
3. **dress up** — dress up — ăn diện; hóa trang (câu gốc: We dressed up for the wedding.)
4. **get at** — get at — ám chỉ (câu gốc: What are you getting at?)
5. **get away** — get away — trốn thoát; đi nghỉ (câu gốc: We need to get away for a few days.)
6. **get over** — get over — vượt qua (bệnh, nỗi buồn) (câu gốc: The team got over the setback quickly.)
7. **get by** — get by — xoay xở, sống qua ngày (câu gốc: We can get by with two testers for now.)
8. **get away with** — get away with — thoát tội (câu gốc: You can't get away with skipping tests forever.)
9. **get on** — get on — lên xe; hòa hợp (Anh) (câu gốc: Get on the bus!)
10. **drop in (on)** — drop in (on) — ghé thăm không báo trước (câu gốc: We dropped in on my aunt.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **Please fill out the bug report template.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). fill out a form (T-S).
2. **Two people dropped out of the training.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). drop out (of school/college); danh từ: dropout.
3. **We dressed up for the wedding.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). dress up (as sb).
4. **What are you getting at?** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). What are you getting at?
5. **We need to get away for a few days.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). get away (from sth); danh từ: getaway.

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *I get up at six every day. / I figure on leaving around six. / I need to get rid of these old boxes.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *“Can I sit here?” “Go ahead.” / Give me back my phone! / Let's get together this weekend.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *The meeting dragged on for hours. / It's easy to get around by bike. / They're giving out free samples.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *Can you drop me off at the station? / Fill out this application, please. / He dropped out of college.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *We dressed up for the wedding. / What are you getting at? / We need to get away for a few days.*
