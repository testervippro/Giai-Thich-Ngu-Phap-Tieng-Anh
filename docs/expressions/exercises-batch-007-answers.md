## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **in the pipeline** — IDIOM-0287 · be in the pipeline.
2. **pull the plug on** — IDIOM-0298 · pull the plug on + N.
3. **come to terms with** — IDIOM-0277 · come to terms with + N.
4. **open a can of worms** — IDIOM-0294 · open (up) a can of worms.
5. **ahead of the curve** — IDIOM-0273 · stay / be ahead of the curve.
6. **to the letter** — IDIOM-0311 · follow sth to the letter.
7. **at a crossroads** — IDIOM-0274 · be at a crossroads.
8. **cut both ways** — IDIOM-0279 · sth cuts both ways.
9. **off the hook** — IDIOM-0291 · be / get off the hook.
10. **on the brink of** — IDIOM-0292 · on the brink of + N.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **pipeline** — IDIOM-0287 · in the pipeline — đang được chuẩn bị
2. **on** — IDIOM-0298 · pull the plug on — khai tử, dừng hẳn
3. **with** — IDIOM-0277 · come to terms with — chấp nhận (điều đau buồn)
4. **worms** — IDIOM-0294 · open a can of worms — khơi ra cả đống rắc rối
5. **curve** — IDIOM-0273 · ahead of the curve — đi trước xu hướng
6. **letter** — IDIOM-0311 · to the letter — đúng từng chữ
7. **crossroads** — IDIOM-0274 · at a crossroads — ở ngã ba đường
8. **ways** — IDIOM-0279 · cut both ways — có cả hai mặt
9. **hook** — IDIOM-0291 · off the hook — thoát khỏi trách nhiệm
10. **of** — IDIOM-0292 · on the brink of — bên bờ

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **take a toll on** — take a toll on — gây tổn hại (câu gốc: On-call duty takes a toll on the team.)
2. **run the gauntlet** — run the gauntlet — chịu trận (câu gốc: The minister had to run the gauntlet of reporters.)
3. **on the verge of** — on the verge of — sắp sửa (câu gốc: We're on the verge of a breakthrough.)
4. **all hands on deck** — all hands on deck — huy động mọi người (câu gốc: It's all hands on deck for the release.)
5. **a red herring** — a red herring — thông tin đánh lạc hướng (câu gốc: That warning in the logs was a red herring.)
6. **throw caution to the wind** — throw caution to the wind — bất chấp rủi ro (câu gốc: We threw caution to the wind and quit our jobs.)
7. **gain ground** — gain ground — dần được ủng hộ (câu gốc: Playwright is gaining ground on Cypress.)
8. **at stake** — at stake — bị đe dọa, đang đặt cược (câu gốc: Our reputation is at stake.)
9. **have a vested interest** — have a vested interest — có lợi ích riêng (câu gốc: He has a vested interest in the deal.)
10. **the final nail in the coffin** — the final nail in the coffin — giọt nước cuối cùng (kết liễu) (câu gốc: The data leak was the final nail in the coffin for the vendor.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **On-call duty takes a toll on the team.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). take a / its toll on + N.
2. **The minister had to run the gauntlet of reporters.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). run the gauntlet (of sth).
3. **We're on the verge of a breakthrough.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). on the verge of + N/V-ing.
4. **It's all hands on deck for the release.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). It's all hands on deck.
5. **That warning in the logs was a red herring.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). be a red herring.
