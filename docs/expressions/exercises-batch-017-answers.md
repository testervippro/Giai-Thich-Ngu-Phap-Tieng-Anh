# Đáp án bài tập đợt 17 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-017.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **kick around** — PRO-PHR-0122 · kick sth around (T-S).
2. **staff up** — PRO-PHR-0082 · staff up (for sth).
3. **allow for** — PRO-PHR-0090 · allow for + N.
4. **send over** — PRO-PHR-0077 · send sth over (T-S).
5. **argue for** — PRO-PHR-0089 · argue for + N/V-ing.
6. **cover off** — PRO-PHR-0114 · cover sth off (T-S) (Anh-Anh công sở).
7. **keep on top of** — PRO-PHR-0121 · keep/stay on top of + N.
8. **plan for** — PRO-PHR-0091 · plan for + N.
9. **slip up** — PRO-PHR-0112 · slip up (I); danh từ: slip-up.
10. **bring up to date** — PRO-PHR-0115 · bring sb up to date (on sth).

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **around** — PRO-PHR-0122 · kick around — bàn bạc sơ bộ (ý tưởng)
2. **up** — PRO-PHR-0082 · staff up — tăng nhân sự
3. **for** — PRO-PHR-0090 · allow for — tính đến (dự phòng)
4. **over** — PRO-PHR-0077 · send over — gửi qua
5. **for** — PRO-PHR-0089 · argue for — lập luận ủng hộ
6. **off** — PRO-PHR-0114 · cover off — xử lý xong (một hạng mục)
7. **of** — PRO-PHR-0121 · keep on top of — nắm chắc, kiểm soát
8. **for** — PRO-PHR-0091 · plan for — lên kế hoạch cho
9. **up** — PRO-PHR-0112 · slip up — sơ suất
10. **date** — PRO-PHR-0115 · bring up to date — cập nhật cho ai

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **lead up to** — lead up to — dẫn tới, giai đoạn trước (câu gốc: In the week leading up to release, we freeze code.)
2. **dial in** — dial in — gọi vào (cuộc họp trực tuyến) (câu gốc: Please dial in five minutes early.)
3. **step aside** — step aside — nhường chỗ, rút lui (câu gốc: I'm stepping aside as release manager.)
4. **lock in** — lock in — chốt cố định (câu gốc: Let's lock in the scope for this sprint.)
5. **measure up** — measure up — đạt yêu cầu (câu gốc: The tool didn't measure up to our needs.)
6. **buy into** — buy into — tin theo, ủng hộ (ý tưởng) (câu gốc: We need the team to buy into TDD.)
7. **put across** — put across — truyền đạt (câu gốc: Use data to put your point across.)
8. **bring in** — bring in — đưa vào; mang lại (doanh thu); mời (câu gốc: We brought in a consultant to audit security.)
9. **close out** — close out — đóng lại, kết thúc (ticket, dự án) (câu gốc: Close out the ticket once QA verifies it.)
10. **win over** — win over — thuyết phục được (câu gốc: The demo won the skeptics over.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **In the week leading up to release, we freeze code.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). in the weeks leading up to + N.
2. **Please dial in five minutes early.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). dial in (to sth).
3. **I'm stepping aside as release manager.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). step aside (for sb).
4. **Let's lock in the scope for this sprint.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). lock sth in (T-S).
5. **The tool didn't measure up to our needs.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). measure up (to sth).

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *Stores staff up for Christmas. / I'd argue for leaving early. / He stepped aside to let younger people lead.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *I think we've covered everything off. / I can't keep on top of my inbox. / Everyone slips up sometimes.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *We kicked around some names for the baby. / Book now to lock in the price. / We need to nail down a date for the party.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *Allow for traffic on the way. / Always plan for the unexpected. / In the days leading up to the wedding…*
5. Gợi ý khung: *Can you send over the photos? / Let me bring you up to date on the gossip. / I'll dial in from home.*
