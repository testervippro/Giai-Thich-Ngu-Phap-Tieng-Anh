# Đáp án bài tập đợt 24 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-024.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **a valid point** — COL-0124 · a valid / fair / good point.
2. **a serious issue** — COL-0103 · a serious / pressing / burning issue.
3. **widen the gap** — COL-0092 · widen / narrow the gap (between…).
4. **a rough idea** — COL-0121 · a rough idea/estimate (of sth).
5. **a key factor** — COL-0108 · a key / major / contributing factor (in sth).
6. **a narrow escape** — COL-0125 · have a narrow escape.
7. **a major problem** — COL-0102 · a major / serious / pressing problem.
8. **increase dramatically** — COL-0135 · increase dramatically/sharply/significantly.
9. **a viable option** — COL-0116 · a viable option/alternative/solution.
10. **strike a balance** — COL-0090 · strike a balance between A and B.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **point** — COL-0124 · a valid point — ý hợp lý
2. **issue** — COL-0103 · a serious issue — vấn đề nghiêm trọng
3. **gap** — COL-0092 · widen the gap — nới rộng khoảng cách
4. **idea** — COL-0121 · a rough idea — ý niệm sơ bộ
5. **factor** — COL-0108 · a key factor — yếu tố then chốt
6. **escape** — COL-0125 · a narrow escape — thoát nạn trong gang tấc
7. **problem** — COL-0102 · a major problem — vấn đề lớn
8. **option** — COL-0116 · a viable option — lựa chọn khả thi
9. **balance** — COL-0090 · strike a balance — cân bằng
10. **of** — COL-0111 · a wide range of — nhiều loại

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **firmly believe** — firmly believe — tin chắc (câu gốc: I firmly believe quality is everyone's job.)
2. **a significant increase** — a significant increase — sự gia tăng đáng kể (câu gốc: We saw a significant increase in errors.)
3. **hold a conversation** — hold a conversation — duy trì cuộc trò chuyện (câu gốc: I can hold a conversation in English.)
4. **seriously consider** — seriously consider — cân nhắc nghiêm túc (câu gốc: We should seriously consider a rewrite.)
5. **a steep price** — a steep price — giá đắt (câu gốc: We paid a steep price for skipping tests.)
6. **vary considerably** — vary considerably — khác nhau đáng kể (câu gốc: Test times vary considerably between runs.)
7. **a great deal of** — a great deal of — rất nhiều (không đếm được) (câu gốc: We spent a great deal of time on setup.)
8. **a tight deadline** — a tight deadline — hạn chót gấp (câu gốc: We're working to a tight deadline.)
9. **highly recommend** — highly recommend — hết lời giới thiệu (câu gốc: I highly recommend this course to new testers.)
10. **a growing concern** — a growing concern — mối lo ngày càng tăng (câu gốc: Flaky tests are a growing concern.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **We test on a wide range of devices.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). a wide / broad range of + N số nhiều.
2. **I firmly believe quality is everyone's job.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). firmly believe (that) + clause.
3. **We saw a significant increase in errors.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). a significant / sharp / dramatic increase in + N.
4. **I can hold a conversation in English.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). hold / have / strike up a conversation.
5. **We should seriously consider a rewrite.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). seriously consider + N/V-ing.

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *That's a valid point. / This is a serious issue. / Can you give me a rough idea of the cost?*
2. Gợi ý khung: *- / It's hard to strike a balance between work and family. / I can hold a conversation in English.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *I firmly believe in second chances. / I'm seriously considering moving abroad. / Your help is greatly appreciated.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *Rent has increased dramatically. / Prices vary considerably.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *Price is a key factor for me. / We had a narrow escape on the motorway. / Traffic is a major problem here.*
