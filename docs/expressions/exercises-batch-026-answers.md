# Đáp án bài tập đợt 26 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-026.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **I'm not entirely convinced** — FIX-0013 · I'm not entirely convinced (that/by…).
2. **by the way** — FIX-0016 · By the way, …; BTW.
3. **to some extent** — FIX-0009 · to some / a certain extent.
4. **state-of-the-art** — COL-0215 · state-of-the-art equipment/technology.
5. **for the sake of** — COL-0202 · for the sake of + N.
6. **eye-opening** — COL-0211 · an eye-opening experience.
7. **labour-intensive** — COL-0214 · labour-intensive (Anh) / labor-intensive (Mỹ).
8. **long-term** — COL-0209 · long-term (effect/plan/goal).
9. **in detail** — COL-0191 · explain/discuss sth in detail.
10. **as it happens** — FIX-0011 · As it happens, + clause.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **convinced** — FIX-0013 · I'm not entirely convinced — tôi chưa hoàn toàn bị thuyết phục
2. **way** — FIX-0016 · by the way — nhân tiện
3. **extent** — FIX-0009 · to some extent — ở mức độ nào đó
4. **of** — COL-0202 · for the sake of — vì lợi ích của
5. **detail** — COL-0191 · in detail — chi tiết
6. **happens** — FIX-0011 · as it happens — tình cờ là
7. **worth** — FIX-0006 · for what it's worth — nói ra để bạn tham khảo
8. **me** — FIX-0007 · if you ask me — nếu hỏi tôi
9. **it** — FIX-0014 · it depends on how you look at it — còn tùy cách nhìn
10. **with** — COL-0203 · in line with — phù hợp với

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **actually** — actually — thật ra (câu gốc: Actually, the test passes now.)
2. **on average** — on average — trung bình (câu gốc: On average, the suite takes 12 minutes.)
3. **advantage of** — advantage of — lợi thế của (câu gốc: The main advantage of Playwright is speed.)
4. **in the face of** — in the face of — đối mặt với (câu gốc: The team stayed positive in the face of delays.)
5. **in the long run** — in the long run — về lâu dài (câu gốc: Automation pays off in the long run.)
6. **with regard to** — with regard to — về vấn đề (câu gốc: With regard to the release, we're on track.)
7. **under pressure** — under pressure — chịu áp lực (câu gốc: The team is under pressure to ship.)
8. **up to a point** — up to a point — ở chừng mực nào đó (câu gốc: It's true up to a point.)
9. **in advance** — in advance — trước (thời hạn) (câu gốc: Please share the agenda in advance.)
10. **in favor of** — in favor of — ủng hộ (câu gốc: I'm in favor of switching to Playwright.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **We ship high-quality software.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). high-quality (product/education).
2. **It's a well-known issue in Chrome.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). well-known (for sth).
3. **We need a more user-friendly error page.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). user-friendly (interface/design).
4. **For what it's worth, the old version was faster.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). For what it's worth, + clause.
5. **If you ask me, we need more testers.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). If you ask me, + clause.

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *They stayed together for the sake of the kids. / Can you explain it in more detail? / Prices rose in line with inflation.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *The gym has state-of-the-art equipment. / The trip was eye-opening. / Farming is labour-intensive.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *By the way, have you seen my keys? / Actually, I've changed my mind. / Anyway, I'd better go.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *I agree to some extent. / For what it's worth, I think you did the right thing. / You're right, up to a point.*
5. Gợi ý khung: *If you ask me, he's lying. / Is it a good deal? It depends on how you look at it. / The way I see it, we have two choices.*
