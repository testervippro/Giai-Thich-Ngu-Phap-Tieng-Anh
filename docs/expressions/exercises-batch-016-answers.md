# Đáp án bài tập đợt 16 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **ramp up** — PRO-PHR-0037 · ramp up (sth) (T-S/I).
2. **jump in** — PRO-PHR-0030 · jump in (I).
3. **talk through** — PRO-PHR-0066 · talk sb through sth; talk sth through.
4. **send out** — PRO-PHR-0076 · send sth out (T-S).
5. **draw on** — PRO-PHR-0054 · draw on + N.
6. **check off** — PRO-PHR-0067 · check sth off (a list) (T-S); Anh: tick off.
7. **home in on** — PRO-PHR-0051 · home in on + N.
8. **chime in** — PRO-PHR-0029 · chime in (with sth).
9. **push through** — PRO-PHR-0040 · push sth through (T-S); push through (I).
10. **carry over** — PRO-PHR-0044 · carry sth over (to/into) (T-S); danh từ: carryover.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **up** — PRO-PHR-0037 · ramp up — tăng mạnh (dần)
2. **in** — PRO-PHR-0030 · jump in — xen vào; tham gia ngay
3. **through** — PRO-PHR-0066 · talk through — bàn kỹ từng bước
4. **out** — PRO-PHR-0076 · send out — gửi đi (hàng loạt)
5. **on** — PRO-PHR-0054 · draw on — dựa vào (kinh nghiệm, nguồn)
6. **off** — PRO-PHR-0067 · check off — đánh dấu đã xong
7. **in** — PRO-PHR-0029 · chime in — xen vào góp ý
8. **through** — PRO-PHR-0040 · push through — thúc cho thông qua; cố vượt qua
9. **over** — PRO-PHR-0044 · carry over — chuyển sang (kỳ sau)
10. **in** — PRO-PHR-0032 · sit in (on) — dự thính

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **clock in/out** — clock in/out — chấm công vào/ra (câu gốc: Remember to clock out before you leave.)
2. **get bogged down (in)** — get bogged down (in) — bị sa lầy (vào chi tiết) (câu gốc: We got bogged down in edge cases.)
3. **phase in** — phase in — áp dụng dần (câu gốc: We'll phase in the new framework team by team.)
4. **farm out** — farm out — giao khoán ra ngoài (câu gốc: We farm out localization testing.)
5. **wind down** — wind down — giảm dần, khép lại; thư giãn (câu gốc: The team is winding down the old product.)
6. **run up against** — run up against — đụng phải (khó khăn) (câu gốc: We ran up against API rate limits.)
7. **fall to** — fall to — thuộc trách nhiệm của (câu gốc: Release notes fall to the QA lead.)
8. **work through** — work through — giải quyết lần lượt; làm xuyên (giờ nghỉ) (câu gốc: Let's work through the backlog one by one.)
9. **single out** — single out — chọn riêng ra (khen/chê) (câu gốc: Let's not single anyone out for the outage.)
10. **smooth over** — smooth over — xoa dịu (bất đồng) (câu gốc: The PM smoothed things over with the client.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **New hires sit in on client calls.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). sit in (on sth).
2. **Remember to clock out before you leave.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). clock in / clock out.
3. **We got bogged down in edge cases.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). get/be bogged down in + N.
4. **We'll phase in the new framework team by team.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). phase sth in (T-S).
5. **We farm out localization testing.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). farm sth out (to sb) (T-S).

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *Check off each item as you pack. / I was tired but pushed through. / I clock in at eight every day.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *The store ramps up staff before Christmas. / Draw on your own experience. / The new rules will be phased in over a year.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *Let's talk it through over coffee. / We sent out the wedding invites. / I tried to smooth things over between them.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *Can I jump in here? / Feel free to chime in anytime. / Can I sit in on your class?*
5. Gợi ý khung: *The speaker homed in on the key problem. / The teacher singled her out for praise. / We zeroed in on a great restaurant.*
