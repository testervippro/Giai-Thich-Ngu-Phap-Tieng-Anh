# Đáp án bài tập đợt 25 *(tự sinh từ các cụm trong đợt)*

[← Quay lại đề bài](expressions/exercises-batch-025.md)

## A. Việt → Anh (10)

1. **interfere with** — COL-0171 · interfere with + N.
2. **effect on** — COL-0185 · an effect / impact / influence on + N.
3. **benefit from** — COL-0159 · benefit from + N/V-ing.
4. **object to** — COL-0164 · object to + N/V-ing.
5. **emphasize (sth)** — COL-0175 · emphasize + N; put emphasis on + N.
6. **bitterly disappointed** — COL-0146 · be bitterly disappointed.
7. **highly unlikely** — COL-0140 · It is highly unlikely that…
8. **reason for** — COL-0184 · the reason for + N / why…
9. **lack (sth)** — COL-0177 · lack + N (động từ); a lack of + N (danh từ).
10. **painfully slow** — COL-0144 · painfully slow/obvious/aware.

## B. Điền vào chỗ trống (10)

1. **with** — COL-0171 · interfere with — can thiệp, cản trở
2. **on** — COL-0185 · effect on — ảnh hưởng lên
3. **from** — COL-0159 · benefit from — hưởng lợi từ
4. **to** — COL-0164 · object to — phản đối
5. **disappointed** — COL-0146 · bitterly disappointed — thất vọng cay đắng
6. **unlikely** — COL-0140 · highly unlikely — rất khó xảy ra
7. **for** — COL-0184 · reason for — lý do cho
8. **slow** — COL-0144 · painfully slow — chậm khủng khiếp
9. **with** — COL-0167 · comply with — tuân thủ
10. **normal** — COL-0145 · perfectly normal — hoàn toàn bình thường

## C. Chọn cụm đúng (10)

1. **deeply rooted** — deeply rooted — ăn sâu bám rễ (câu gốc: The tradition is deeply rooted in our culture.)
2. **approve of** — approve of — tán thành (câu gốc: My parents don't approve of my boyfriend.)
3. **fundamentally different** — fundamentally different — khác căn bản (câu gốc: Unit and e2e tests are fundamentally different.)
4. **heavily dependent on** — heavily dependent on — phụ thuộc nhiều vào (câu gốc: The tests are heavily dependent on test data.)
5. **widely used** — widely used — được dùng rộng rãi (câu gốc: Selenium is still widely used.)
6. **specialize in** — specialize in — chuyên về (câu gốc: I specialize in performance testing.)
7. **attitude towards** — attitude towards — thái độ đối với (câu gốc: His attitude towards testing has changed.)
8. **derive from** — derive from — bắt nguồn từ (câu gốc: Many English words derive from Latin.)
9. **focus on** — focus on — tập trung vào (câu gốc: Let's focus on the critical bugs.)
10. **increase in** — increase in — sự gia tăng về (câu gốc: We saw an increase in test failures.)

## D. Viết lại / đặt câu (5)

1. **The app must comply with GDPR.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). comply with + rules/regulations.
2. **A few warnings are perfectly normal.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). perfectly normal/fine/clear/reasonable.
3. **The tradition is deeply rooted in our culture.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). deeply rooted / deep-rooted (in sth).
4. **My parents don't approve of my boyfriend.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). approve of + N/V-ing.
5. **Unit and e2e tests are fundamentally different.** — Câu mẫu (tự soạn). fundamentally / completely different (from sth).

## E. Nói (5)

1. Gợi ý khung: *Phones interfere with sleep. / You'd benefit from more sleep. / I object to being called lazy.*
2. Gợi ý khung: *I was bitterly disappointed with the result. / It's highly unlikely he'll come. / The Wi-Fi here is painfully slow.*
3. Gợi ý khung: *Coffee has no effect on me. / What's the reason for the delay? / She has a great attitude towards work.*
4. Gợi ý khung: *-*
5. Gợi ý khung: *I want to emphasize this point. / He lacks confidence. / You must comply with the rules.*
