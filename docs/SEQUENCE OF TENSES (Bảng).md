# SỰ PHỐI HỢP THÌ TRONG CÁC CẤU TRÚC CÂU KHÁC (SEQUENCE OF TENSES)

### Kiến thức cần nhớ:

- Định nghĩa và công thức của các thì đã học ở Chương 2.

- Các quy tắc phối hợp thì thường gặp.

## Mục tiêu học tập:

- Nắm rõ các thì nào thường hay xuất hiện chung trong một câu.

- Biết cách sử dụng các liên từ chỉ thời gian khi phối hợp thì.

- Biết cách chia thì trong mệnh đề If, Wish/ If only, và câu tường thuật (Reported Speech).

Phối hợp thi là nguyên tắc yêu cầu sự nhất quán giữa các thì trong câu có nhiều mệnh đề. Một câu có thể bao gồm một mệnh đề chính (main clause) và một hoặc nhiều mệnh đề phụ (subordinate clause). Khi một câu có hai hoặc nhiều mệnh đề, động từ trong các mệnh đề phải phù hợp với nhau về thì.

## I. Mệnh đề chính và Mệnh đề phụ (Main clause and Subordinate clause)

Mệnh đề chính (Main Clause) là một mệnh đề độc lập, có thể dùng một mình và diễn đạt một ý hoàn chỉnh.

Mệnh đề phụ (Subordinate Clause) là một mệnh đề không thể đứng một mình mà phải đi kèm với mệnh đề chính để tạo thành một câu có nghĩa.

☑ She was happy because she passed the exam.

Mệnh đề chính: She was happy.

Mệnh đề phụ: because she passed the exam.

♦ I will call you later when I get home.

Mệnh đề chính: I will call you later.

Mệnh đề phụ: when I get home.

## II. Các quy tắc phối hợp thì quan trọng (Important Rules of Sequence of Tenses)

a. Khi mệnh đề chính ở các thì HIỆN TẠI hoặc TƯƠNG LAI → Mệnh đề phụ có thể ở các thì sau:

| MỆNH ĐỀ CHÍNH | MỆNH ĐỀ PHỤ | VÍ DỤ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present Simple | Present Simple | He drinks coffee whenever he feels tired. |
| Present Simple | Present Continuous | She tells me that everyone is preparing for the presentation. |
| Present Simple | Present Perfect | Ralph feels very happy because he has achieved his goals. |
| Present Simple | Future Simple | They study English diligently since they will move to Canada soon. |
| Present Simple | "Be going to" | We clean the house thoroughly because guests are going to stay overnight. |
| Present Continuous | Present Simple (thường nói về sự thật, chân lý hiển nhiên, hoặc thói quen) | Bobby is explaining that he always checks his email every morning. |
| Present Continuous | Present Continuous | Miley is cleaning the house while her sister is cooking dinner. |
| Present Continuous | Present Perfect | I am reading the book that you have recommended. |
| Present Continuous | Future Simple | My family is saving money because we will buy a house next year. |
| Present Continuous | "Be going to" | Now we are preparing extra food for the cats since we are going to be away this weekend. |
| Present Perfect / Present Perfect Continuous | Present Simple | I have forgotten what he looks like. |
| Present Perfect / Present Perfect Continuous | Present Continuous | We haven't finished the green project yet because Gary is still working on it. |
| Present Perfect / Present Perfect Continuous | Past Simple (có thời điểm cụ thể) | She has lived in Paris since she was a child. |
| Present Perfect / Present Perfect Continuous | Present Perfect • ít thông dụng, thường dùng để diễn tả sự kiện vẫn chưa kết thúc ở mệnh đề trạng ngữ chỉ thời gian. | I haven't been able to concentrate since I have had this headache. (cơn đau đầu vẫn còn tiếp diễn) |
| Future Simple | Present Simple | She will phone me when she arrives. |
| Future Simple | Present Continuous | I will join if you are still playing basketball for that team. |
| Future Simple | Present Perfect | Kevin will meet you after he has done all his tasks. |
| Be going to | Present Simple | I am going to leave when the rain stops. |
| Be going to | Present Perfect | She is going to call him immediately when she has received the package. |

b. Khi mệnh đề chính ở các thì QUÁ KHỨ → Mệnh đề phụ có thể ở các thì sau:

| MỆNH ĐỀ CHÍNH | MỆNH ĐỀ PHỤ | VÍ DỤ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Past Simple | Past Simple | They visited the museum because they loved history. |
| Past Simple | Past Continuous | He discovered the solution while he was walking in the park. |
| Past Simple | Past Perfect | I came to see her, but she had already gone. |
| Past Continuous | Past Simple | She was sleeping when the phone rang. |
| Past Continuous | Past Continuous | While I was studying, my roommate was listening to music. |
| Past Continuous | Past Perfect | Laura was feeling tired because she hadn't slept well the night before. |
| Past Perfect / Past Perfect Continuous | Past Simple | We had already eaten dinner before she arrived. |
| Would + V bare-in | Past Simple | They would go to the beach if the weather was nicer. |
| Tương lai trong quá khứ | Past Perfect | If he hadn't missed his flight last night, he would be here with us now. |

Các cấu trúc khác dùng để phối hợp thì trong tiếng Anh

Câu gián tiếp (Reported Speech)

Khi tường thuật lại lời nói của người khác, nếu động từ tường thuật ở thì quá khứ, ta cần phải lùi thì (backshift).

Quy tác lùi thì:

| Câu trực tiếp (Direct Speech) | Câu gián tiếp (Reported Speech) |
| --- | --- |
| Present Simple → Past Simple | "I like ice cream," she said. → She said (that) she liked ice cream. |
| Present Continuous → Past Continuous | "Where are you going?" he asked. → He asked me where I was going. |
| Present Perfect → Past Perfect | "Have you finished your homework?" she asked them. → She asked if/ whether they had finished their homework. |
| Past Simple → Past Perfect | "I met him yesterday," she said. → She told me (that) she had met him the previous day. |
| Future Simple (will) → Would | "I will call you," he said. → He said (that) he would call me. |

* xem chi tiết cách lùi thì ở phần Câu tường thuật (Reported Speech) trong Chương 4. Câu điều kiện (Conditional Sentences)

Các câu điều kiện cũng yêu cầu sự phối hợp giữa các thì trong mệnh đề If và mệnh đề chính.

| Loại | Mệnh đề If | Mệnh đề chính | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Loại 0 (Zero Conditional) | Present Simple | Present Simple | If you heat water to 100°C, it boils. |
| Loại 1 (First Conditional) | Present Simple | Future Simple (will) | If it rains, we will stay at home. |
| Loại 2 (Second Conditional) | Past Simple | Would + V (bare-inf.) | If I were rich, I would travel the world. |
| Loại 3 (Third Conditional) | Past Perfect | Would have + past participle | If you had studied, you would have passed the exam. |

xem chi tiết ở phần Câu điều kiện (Conditional Sentences) trong Chương 4.

##### Câu ước (Wish & If only Sentences)

Cấu trúc này dùng để diễn tả mong ước về một điều KHÔNG có thật ở hiện tại, quá khứ hoặc tương lai.

| Thời gian ước muốn | Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hiện tại | S + wish(es) + Past Simple | I wish I was/ were taller. |
| Quá khứ | S + wish(es) + Past Perfect | She wishes she had studied harder. |
| Tương lai | S + wish(es) + Would + V barc-inf. | I wish it would stop raining. |

##### LƯU Ý:

Nếu chủ ngữ là I, he, she, it và danh từ số ít, ta có thể sử dụng cả was và were.

Ex. She wishes she was/ were a doctor.

If only it wasn't/ weren't raining.

- Để thể hiện cảm giác phiền phức về một điều gì đó, ta sử dụng wish + would(n't)

Ex: I wish the neighbour wouldn't play loud music.

He wishes his son wouldn't interrupt him when he is working.

* xem chi tiết ở phần Mệnh đề sau Wish và If Only (Clauses after Wish and If Only) trong Chương 3.

- Câu so sánh thời gian khi sử dụng với Before, After, When, By the time, As soon as Những cấu trúc này cũng đòi hỏi sự phối hợp thì đúng giữa hai mệnh đề. Lưu ý, có thể đặt các liên từ chỉ thời gian ở đầu câu hoặc giữa câu, và liên từ luôn ĐI VỚI mệnh đề phụ.

| Liên từ | Mệnh đề phụ | Mệnh đề chính | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before | Past Simple | Past Perfect | Before she went out, she had turned off the lights. |
| After | Past Perfect | Past Simple | After Brian had left, we closed the door. |
| When | Past Simple | Past Continuous | I was watching TV when he called. |
| When | Past Continuous | Past Simple | When she was walking to the store, she met an old friend. |
| While | Past Continuous | Past Simple | While I was reading a book, the light went out. |
| While | Past Continuous | Past Continuous | We were painting the fence while Tom was cooking lunch. |
| By the time | Past Simple | Past Perfect | By the time I arrived, they had left. |
| By the time | Present Simple | Future Perfect Tense | By the time he graduates, he will have completed all his coursework. |
| As soon as | Present Simple | Future Simple | As soon as he arrives, we will start the meeting. |
| As soon as | Past Simple | Past Simple | He started eating as soon as the food was placed on the table. |
| As soon as | Past Perfect | Past Simple | He called his parents as soon as he had arrived at the hotel. |
| Until | Present Simple | Future Simple | I will wait here until you come back. |
| Until | Present Simple | Future Perfect | We will have done the cleaning until the guests arrive. |
| Until | Past Simple | Past Simple | Josh waited patiently until the bus came. |
| Until | Past Simple | Past Perfect | They had lived in that house for ten years until they moved to Australia. |
